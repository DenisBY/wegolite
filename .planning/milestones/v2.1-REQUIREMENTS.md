# Requirements: Wegolite

**Defined:** 2026-11-13
**Milestone:** v2.1 World Population Base
**Core Value:** A player can launch, fly, dock, and complete a profitable trade run between two star systems entirely in the browser — faithful to Oolite's rules, data, and feel.

**Scope rule:** the necessary technical minimum for an inert, populated world (owner, 2026-11-13: "less, but better"). Each requirement is trimmed to what Phase 12 consumes. Requirement IDs reused from the v2.0 archive (`.planning/milestones/v2.0-REQUIREMENTS.md`) keep their ID but carry the trimmed text below; the parts cut from them are listed under Future Requirements with new IDs.

## v2.1 Requirements

### Foundation hardening

- [x] **FOUND-15**: A world-step AI module that throws is contained: the entity is handled by a recorded rule, the step completes, and the world hash stays deterministic (Phase 8 review WR-01)
- [x] **FOUND-16**: `spawnEntity` refuses malformed input with a reason instead of throwing, and a refused spawn does not advance the id counter (Phase 8 review IN-02)

### Content

- [x] **DATA-10**: The ship models and textures of the six populator roles (traders, pirates, police Vipers, hunters, shuttles, scavengers) are compiled from the pinned Oolite snapshot with provenance in `ASSETS.md`/`NOTICE`, behind the drift gate
- [x] **DATA-11**: Each of those ships carries the fields the inert world uses, taken from `shipdata.plist`: roles, AI name, scan class, model, and its mass computed from a build-time octree as Oolite does (`density * 20 * octree volume`), checked by worked examples for the Cobra Mk III, the Coriolis station and the Viper; the Coriolis octree mass is compared with the 455,223,200 used by jump blockage, and one value is kept by owner decision if they differ
- [x] **DATA-13**: NPC models and textures are lazily loaded outside the first-load set, and the cold-start transfer stays under the 9,437,184-byte ceiling

### Saves

- [x] **SAVE-07**: Saves move to a minimal schema v3 that adds only the world epoch, the world counters and the next entity id; v1 and v2 saves migrate in order, older builds treat a v3 save as an inert future-version save, and the worst-case v3 save stays under the 65,536-character cap
- [x] **SAVE-08**: NPCs are not saved: loading a save regenerates the inert world at the saved leg anchor (the docked station or the witchpoint arrival), hashing identically to the world a fresh arrival at that anchor builds; a save taken in flight resumes at its leg anchor, never mid-leg

### World

- [x] **WORLD-15**: After launch and after an arrival, a first populator places inert NPC ships of the six roles; they are drawn with their Oolite models and shown on the scanner without a new HUD element, a populated scene stays within the 50 draw-call budget, and `nullAI` ships stay inert as in Oolite
- [x] **WORLD-10**: Mass lock and hyperspace jump blockage take nearby NPC ships into account as in Oolite, and the torus refusal message is correct when the torus never engaged
- [x] **WORLD-11**: The simulation keeps running while the galactic chart is open in flight, as in Oolite (supersedes the v1 freeze), including a running hyperspace countdown
- [x] **WORLD-14**: A hyperspace jump clears every non-static entity at jump commit, and the destination is populated deterministically on arrival: the same route and seed give the same ships, ids and world hash
- [x] **WORLD-16**: With traffic on, launch, manual docking, the docking computer and torus flight behave as in v1; browser tests run with traffic off by default and every existing browser test passes unchanged, except the v1 chart-freeze check in `tests/e2e/hyperspace.spec.ts`, which WORLD-11 inverts (owner, 2026-11-13), and the opt-in `traffic=1` entity count in `tests/e2e/tick-hook.spec.ts`, which expires once the populator exists (owner, 2026-11-14)
