- `session(search, populated, hudRouting, damage = false)`: with `damage` the session loads the real compiled equipment catalogue, the probe publisher's `commitProfile` replaces `ctx.core.profile` under the station controller's identity check, and the router's `damageFlow` is the real `createDamageFlow(ctx, { commitProfile, messages })` behind the same recorder as `hudRouting`.
