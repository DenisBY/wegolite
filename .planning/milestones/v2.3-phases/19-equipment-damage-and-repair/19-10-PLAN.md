## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-01:** the simulation emits events and the app commits the profile in one atomic update (roadmap SC1).
- **D-04:** the cargo loss goes through the same event path and the same atomic update as equipment damage; the HUD shows `"@-destroyed"` with the commodity's display name, 4.5 s, after clearing the previous message.
- **D-07:** `"@-damaged"` verbatim, 4.5 s, after clearing the previous message.
- **Claude's discretion:** the router rows and the new subscriber (research A5's `damageFlow`); `stationFlow` stays "launched, launchComplete and docked" only. `damageFlow` also prints the two messages, gated on its own `commitProfile` result, and the two kinds are routed to it alone (checker iteration 2, T-19-17). Research and `19-PATTERNS.md` suggested routing the messages through `hudMessages`; that subscriber runs after the commit and cannot tell whether a cargo loss was applied (the hold after a refused loss looks like any other hold), and its planned "key is in `damagedEquipment`" test would also print again for a repeated event. `hyperspaceFlow` (`hyperspaceRefused`) is the precedent for a gameplay subscriber that owns its HUD message.
