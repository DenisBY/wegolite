## Float32 choices

- GLfloat (rounded with `Math.fround` at every step): `m1`, `m2`, `v2b`, `v1a`, `loc`, every `Vector` op (`dot_product`, `magnitude2`, `vector_multiply_scalar`, `vector_subtract`), the dam expressions before widening, `t`; braking `velmag`, `velmag2`, `rate`, `thrust_factor`, `dt_thrust`, `vscale`; shields and energy; blast `desired_range`, `amount`, `cr` and `cr x cr`, `p2`, the `addImpactMoment` fraction and `howmuch / mass`.
- Double: `hploc`, positions and `applyVelocity` integration, `dam1`/`dam2` after assignment, the blast's `d2` (`magnitude2(p2)` widened minus `ecr x ecr`) and `moment`, `HPdistance2`.
- `sqrt` is the double function rounded back on assignment. No fused multiply-add assumed (the x86-64 SSE baseline has none).

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Buoy subentity collision radius missing from the input facts**
- **Found during:** Task 1/2 (the subentity blast source needs its radius)
- **Fix:** derived in-oracle from `oolite_buoy_subents.dat` with OOMesh's rule (25.822647094726562 m); the same rule reproduces the plan's buoy radius 59.13554763793945 from `oolite_buoy.dat`, which validates it. Recorded in the fixtures' derivations.
- **Commit:** `2607b04`, `307049d`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Absolute tolerance was vacuous for small values**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (a blast fixture with d2 near 1e4 and dv near 7e-3 got tolerance 0.01)
- **Fix:** every fixture family compares relatively, `|a - b| <= 1e-6 x |b| + 1e-9`, defined once in `schema.ts` (`withinTolerance`) and tested for non-vacuity; the collision fixtures changed only their `tolerance` field.
- **Commit:** `307049d`

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] Unreachable float boundary**
- **Found during:** Task 1. The plan asks for damage on "the floats either side of 0.05"; the float immediately below f(0.05) cannot come out of `f(X / 5e7)`. The below-side fixtures use the largest reachable value, 0.04999999329447746, and the derivation explains why.
- **Commit:** `2607b04`

**4. [Rule 1 - Bug] Line numbers in the plan's interface list are off by one in places**
- The C has `t` at 11138 (not 11139), `adjustVelocity` at 11173-11176, `dam2 > 0.05` at 11123, the blast range and amount at 8124-8125, the player's thrust factor and braking at 3378-3404, `energy -= amount` at 11651. The fixtures cite the checked lines.
