## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-06 / criterion 4:** fixtures first, by a separate agent, without access to `src/sim/npc`.
- **D-07:** whole-tick shot timer with the NPC `>=`; float32 temperature; cooling `max(temp - 6 x dt, 0)` per tick; cost and heat on a fired shot (`ShipEntity.m:9967-9975`). The float32 width of temperature is settled here against the source.
- **D-04:** the range roll stays as in Oolite: `range > randf() x weaponRange x (accuracy + 7.5)` refuses (9949), one draw.
- **D-15:** `missedShots` enters `currentAimTolerance` as `basic_aim /= 1 + missedShots / 4` for accuracy >= 0 (9769).
- **Advisor decision 1:** the rule's period (`round(rate x 120)` ticks) is kept and the literal Oolite frame count is recorded as a measured divergence, derived here independently and compared with the advisor's numbers only afterwards.
