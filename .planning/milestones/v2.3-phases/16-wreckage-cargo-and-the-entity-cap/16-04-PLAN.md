## Phase 16 decisions this plan implements

- **D16-11 (wreck lifetime, LOW confidence):** a wreck spawns with `maxEnergy = energy = lifetime / 2`; each world step `stepDebris` subtracts `wreckHeatDrain(ageSeconds, dt) = dt x (1000 - 10 x ageSeconds)` while that temperature is above 256 (else 0), with `ageSeconds = (tick - spawnTick) x dt`, floors the energy at 0 in the same write and calls `killShip(world, id, NO_ENTITY, events)` at 0. The `1e-8 T` cooling term is dropped (below 1e-5 relative). Arithmetic from the C, never measured in a running Oolite; plan 16-09 records it in FIDELITY.md and the measurement todo.
- **D16-12 (order, second part):** after the pods: (3) when `canFragment` and `n_alloys > 0`: the `n_wreckage` draw (only when `n_alloys < 3`, as the C), then per wreck i: 7 octant draws, lifetime, model, expected mass, quaternion (4), push; then the `n_alloys = u x n_alloys` draw; (4) per plate: box (3), jitter (3), velocity (3), quaternion (4); (5) the blast in ascending id over the live pieces. Every piece's draws precede its spawn attempt.
- **D16-16:** the wreck model is `wrecks[Math.floor(u x wrecks.length)]` (uniform over the five, research A2); an empty list still takes the draw and spawns nothing.
- **D16-17 (fixed octant budget):** each wreck takes `OCTANT_DRAWS_PER_WRECK = MAX_OCTREE_DEPTH` (7) octant draws whatever the octree's shape and whether `ctx.octrees` holds the victim, consumed one per level descended; Oolite draws one per level. With no octree the point is the origin (the wreck starts at the victim's centre). This keeps the stream position independent of the lazily loaded table (D-15).
- **D16-04 (OC-4):** the blast is ported for pods, plates and wrecks only, each component clamped at `MAX_ENTITY_VELOCITY` (a piece near the centre hits the 0.1 floor: 6,458 m/s for a pod, 23,463 m/s for a plate, clamped). Ships and the player are not pushed: the largest kick a non-overlapping NPC neighbour could get is 0.027 m/s (a Krait victim on a Shuttle) and the player under 0.002 m/s (research). A wreck's mass is `model.mass x (radius / model.radius)^3`, which equals its expected mass because the scale is uniform; a pod or plate keeps its model mass.
- **D16-07 / D16-24:** one plate look (`alloy`) and no plate role draw.
- **D16-05 (wrecks):** wreckage spawns only while `liveEntityCount < WRECKAGE_ENTITY_LIMIT` (and below `DEBRIS_ENTITY_LIMIT`), so at most 127 live before a wreck spawns. Divergence recorded: Oolite adds at most one plate above 0.95 (`ShipEntity.m:8077-8080`); here no piece spawns at or above 243.2.
- **D16-15 (wrecks):** kind `wreckage`, scan class `CLASS_NO_DRAW` (`shipdata.plist:4243`), `ai` null, `energyRechargeRate` 0; Oolite's `max_energy = 20000` is irrelevant because `setEnergy:lifetime` and no recharge apply. Plates as pods (`CLASS_CARGO`, energy 1).
- **D16-20:** the proximity pass is unchanged: debris has no AI and a mass of 0 there (no flight or mass row), so a pair with a piece returns early and a fresh wreck never alerts a ship (in Oolite a wreck above 2,000 kg could, for its 0.05 to 0.7 s; recorded).
- **D16-03 (OC-3):** the test pilot's scan excludes cargo and wreckage (`TEST_PILOT_SCAN_KINDS`), hash-neutral for every existing world (none holds debris).
