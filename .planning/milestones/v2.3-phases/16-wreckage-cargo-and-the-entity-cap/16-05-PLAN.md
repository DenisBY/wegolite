## Phase 16 decisions this plan implements

- **D16-22 (explosion data):** `explosions.json` holds only the settings the render draws: `EXPLOSION_KEYS = ["oolite-default-ship-explosion"]`, the default every compiled hull uses (all nine records have `explosionType` null, so `becomeExplosion` takes the default cloud plus the flash, `ShipEntity.m:7956-7963`). The wreckage, fragment and spark entries are not compiled because nothing draws them (no burn-out cloud, no fragment sprites, D16-08), and their textures are not vendored. The flash has no plist entry: its constants come from `OOFlashEffectEntity.m` in plan 16-07. The record keeps the authored `count` (25); the render caps it at 10 (D16-08, plan 16-07).
- **D16-23 (hull box):** `boundingBox` on the NPC record is `{ min: [x, y, z], max: [x, y, z] }`, the float32 extremes of the hull `.dat` vertices in Oolite's model axes (as Oolite's drawable box, `OOEntityWithDrawable.m:55`); the sim converts it with `toWegoliteLocal` (D16-18). No subentity contributes (none of the nine hulls has a solid one).
- **D16-08:** only the cloud and flash particle textures are shipped.
