<objective>
Write the computed masses into the compiled records and enter them in the constants ledger (DATA-11, D-01, D-05, roadmap criteria 1, 2 and 4).

Purpose: Phase 12 reads one `mass` number per ship from the lazily loaded `ships-npc.json` and from `ships-client.json` for the player Cobra and the Coriolis; nothing else of the octree may ship. The content golden and `assets:check` become the mass golden that fails when a mesh or an octree rule changes a mass (D-05).

Decisions this plan applies without asking (both are within D-05 and leave the owner's open questions alone):
- `ships.json` keeps no mass (10-RESEARCH Open Question 4, option (a)): the client test compares entries with `mass` removed and asserts the masses separately, so the 706 KB build catalogue and its golden stay untouched.
- The masses map is computed once in `generate.ts` for the nine NPC keys, the two client keys and the two buoy keys. The buoy's mass is computed here (D-02) but not written anywhere: where it is stored is the owner question of plan 10-03, implemented in plan 10-04.
- The blockage constant in `src/sim/hyperspace/countdown.ts` is not touched here; D-04 is plan 10-03.

Output: the schema and emitter changes, regenerated `ships-npc.json`, `ships-client.json` and `manifest.json`, the updated golden and tests, FIDELITY.md rows E-27 to E-30 and the constants ledger, and the NOTICE wording.
</objective>
