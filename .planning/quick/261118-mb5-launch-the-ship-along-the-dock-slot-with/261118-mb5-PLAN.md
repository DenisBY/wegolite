Launch the player ship along the dock slot, as Oolite does. Owner report (2026-11-18): the ship exits across the slot
instead of along it, and the original launch was already wrong.

Oolite's player launch goes through `DockEntity launchShip:` (`PlayerEntity.m:6467`). At `DockEntity.m:819-827` (pin
7437f22) it builds the launch orientation from the station and dock orientations. When
`(port_dimensions.x < port_dimensions.y) ^ (bb.max.x - bb.min.x < bb.max.y - bb.min.y)`, it rotates that orientation
by pi/2 about the launch vector, "to account for the slot being at 90 degrees to vertical". The Coriolis slot is
taller than wide (`src/content/generated/dock-port.json` port width/height) and the Cobra is wider than tall (the
`ship` bbox in the same file), so the rule rotates. Wegolite's `beginLaunch` sets `orientation = stationBaseQuat` and
skips the rotation. That pose leaves the wings across the narrow side of the slot, wider than the slot itself, which
is the pose PROJECT.md's Key Decision "Leave the Phase 3 launch orientation untouched" kept on purpose. This task
supersedes that decision.

Facts established during planning (cite them; do not re-derive unless a check disagrees):

- Oolite's quaternion convention is the conjugate of local-to-world. At 7437f22, `vector_forward_from_quaternion`
  and `vector_right_from_quaternion` return the third and first rows of the standard rotation matrix
  (`src/Core/OOQuaternion.m:50-150`). `quaternion_multiply` is the Hamilton product (`OOQuaternion.m:30-38`), and
  `quaternion_rotate_about_axis` right-multiplies by (cos a/2, axis sin a/2) (`OOQuaternion.m:345-358`).
  Conjugating `q1 * q2`, where the axis is q1's own world forward, gives the local-to-world rotation R(q1) times a
  rotation of -pi/2 about the ship's local nose axis. Check with q1 = identity: Oolite's right vector is (0,-1,0) and
  its up vector is (1,0,0).
- The content mirror is `(x, y, -z)` for ship and station meshes alike (`tools/oolite-ingest/convert/glb.ts:19`,
  `tools/oolite-ingest/convert/dock-port.ts` `stationBox`/`shipBox`). The mirror commutes with any rotation about z.
  The Coriolis dock subentity has identity orientation (`shipdata.plist`, `oolite_coriolis_dock`), so the Wegolite
  port frame (`src/sim/dock/port-frame.ts`) is Oolite's dock frame mirrored. Wegolite's `mul`, `rotate` and
  `axisAngle` (`src/sim/math.ts:46-83`) are the standard Hamilton product, q·v·q* and the right-hand axis-angle.
  So the faithful Wegolite pose is `stationBaseQuat * axisAngle(0, 0, 1, -pi/2)`, normalised. It maps local +X to
  port -j, local +Y to port +i and local -Z to port k.
- Oolite uses `[ship boundingBox]` for the launch and `totalBoundingBox` for docking acceptance. For the Cobra both
  equal `DockGeometry.ship`.
- `stepLaunch` moves the ship along `rotate(orientation, FORWARD)` and rolls it about local +Z (D-10). A twist about
  the same axis changes neither the exit path nor the roll, so only the orientation moves during launching.
- Docking (`src/sim/dock/corridor.ts`) already follows Oolite's aperture rule (`DockEntity.m:631-653`): the corridor
  half-extents are ww along i and hh along j, and a ship is in the lane only with its wings along j. The launched pose
  is now in that lane. Docking code is not changed.
- No NPC launches from a station (`src/sim/world/populate.ts:17-18` places traffic at the aegis, and
  `src/sim/npc/proximity.ts:33` says no NPC docks or launches). No file under `src/sim/npc/**`, `src/sim/world/**`
  or `src/content/generated/ships-npc.json` changes, so the NPC acceptance run is not due under the AGENTS.md rule.
  If any of those files does change, it becomes due on the remote.
- The docked pose (`src/sim/flight/state.ts:119`, `src/sim/dock/step.ts:176`, `src/sim/dock/computer.ts:61`) and the
  poisoned-orientation repair (`src/sim/flight/sanitize.ts:61`) stay `stationBaseQuat`. They are not launch poses,
  `beginLaunch` overwrites the docked pose, and no save field changes.
