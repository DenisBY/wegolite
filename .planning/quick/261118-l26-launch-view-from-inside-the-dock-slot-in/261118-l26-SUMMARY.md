# Quick 261118-l26: launch view from inside the dock slot Summary

The launch now draws the live fore cockpit view from 0.8 of the station's dock depth inside the slot, eased out
through the mouth onto the cockpit pose at handover. The view is presentation-only: the sim launch, goldens and
hashes are unchanged, and the tunnel overlay and the external cinematic are deleted.

## What was built

- `src/render/launch-camera.ts`
  - `computeSlotLaunchCamera(layout, slotDepth, position, orientation, elapsed)` takes the fore cockpit pose and
    subtracts `stationToPlanet * (0.8 * slotDepth + 31) * (1 - smoothstep(elapsed / LAUNCH_DURATION))`.
  - `launchViewElapsed(launchElapsed, alpha)` gives the launch time of the interpolated pose, clamped and
    finite-checked.
  - `launchViewStationAngle(layout, elapsed)` gives the station angle at that time. The sim invariant behind it,
    `stationAngle == rate * launchElapsed` while launching, is pinned by a test at 1, 24, 120 and 239 steps.
  - The module holds no Coriolis literal; a test guards that.
  - A missing, non-finite or non-positive depth falls back to the plain cockpit pose.
  - The external cinematic, its basis helper and its offset constants are deleted.
- `src/app/session/render-driver.ts`
  - While launching, the camera is the slot path with `ctx.core.dockGeometry?.port.depth`, read live.
  - The station is rolled at the pose's interpolated launch time.
  - The Cobra is hidden in every mode.
  - The HUD canvas is hidden while docked or launching, and no HUD model is built for those frames.
  - `launchSequence` is no longer a dependency of the driver.
- `src/app/launch-sequence.ts` is now the skip gate only. The phase type, `launchPhaseAt`, the two boundary
  constants and the `phase` getter are gone, and `advance` returns `void`.
- `src/render/break-pattern.ts` (the tunnel overlay) is deleted, along with its runtime field, its construction and
  its disposal. The two numbered teardown lists in `start-session.ts` no longer list it.
- `tests/e2e/launch.spec.ts`:
  - The hull and `#hud` are hidden while launching, and `#hud` is visible after handover.
  - The page overlay's `#title` and `#status` are hidden while launching.
  - The fourth test is renamed "the launch view freezes with pause".
- Records:
  - CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]`: a new `### Changed` entry.
  - PROJECT.md: a Key Decisions row.
  - FIDELITY.md: a Mechanics row and the E-113 excerpt.
  - `.planning/todos/pending/2026-11-18-per-system-main-station-types.md`: one sentence added. The todo is now
    committed; it was untracked before.
  - `.planning/todos/pending/2026-11-18-draw-the-hud-while-launching.md`: a new todo.

- **Found during:** Task 2, while reviewing the HUD-canvas change against `index.html`.

  `#hud:not([hidden]) ~ #overlay ...` (quick 261118-cyn, D-02). Hiding the HUD canvas while launching would have made
