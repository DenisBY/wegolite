station's dock slot and flies out through it (owner decision 2026-11-18, option 1, locked). This is a deliberate
Wegolite divergence from Oolite, which hides the world and draws ten slot-shaped rings while launching
(`Universe.m:2895-2913` at 7437f22, called from `PlayerEntity.m:6471`).

Purpose: the owner wants the launch to show the station, not an abstract pattern. The simulation stays Oolite's.

Output: a pure slot launch view in `src/render/launch-camera.ts` wired into the render driver; the overlay module,
its phase timeline and the external cinematic deleted; unit and browser tests updated; frames captured on the remote
for the owner; CHANGELOG, PROJECT.md and FIDELITY.md rows; `verify-full` green on the remote.

Design questions, settled from the code (answer and evidence):

1. Where the ship is. The sim parks the ship at the dock mouth, not inside: `beginLaunch` sets
   `position = dockMouthPosition(layout)` (`src/sim/flight/launch.ts:49`), which is station centre plus
   `stationToPlanet * dockOffset` (`src/sim/flight/state.ts:203-208`), and `stepLaunch` moves it outward at
   `launchSpeed = 0.5 * maxSpeed` (`src/sim/flight/config.ts:43`; 175 m/s for the Cobra's 350) for 2 s. The fore
   cockpit is 31 m ahead of the hull centre (`VIEW_OFFSETS.fore`), so today's cockpit pose starts 31 m outside the
   mouth and ends about 381 m out. Oolite does the same: `DockEntity.m:812-837` places the ship at the dock
   subentity's absolute position with half its top speed. Choice: (a), a presentation-only camera path. Changing
   the sim would move a faithful mechanic, every launch hash and golden, and the handover pose, and buys nothing the
   view cannot show. No file under `src/sim` changes, so no golden or hash pin moves and the NPC acceptance run is
   not due.
2. The path. Camera = the fore cockpit pose of the interpolated sim pose, pulled back along the slot axis
   (`layout.stationToPlanet`) by `pullback(t) = (LAUNCH_VIEW_START_FRACTION * slotDepth + 31) * (1 - smoothstep(t /
   LAUNCH_DURATION))`, with `LAUNCH_VIEW_START_FRACTION = 0.8` and `slotDepth = dockGeometry.port.depth`. At t = 0
   the camera is 0.8 of the slot depth inside the mouth (200 m for the Coriolis' 250 m); smoothstep has zero slope
   at both ends, so the view starts at the ship's speed and lands on the cockpit pose with the ship's velocity. With
   the Cobra's data the camera is about 158 m inside at 0.2 s, crosses the mouth near 0.73 s, is about 226 m out at
   1.4 s and 362 m at 1.9 s, which matches the owner's capture times (deep inside, mouth, outside, merged). The
   orientation is the ship's own (fore view), so the camera rolls with the hull.
3. Station data, not Coriolis numbers (coordinator, 2026-11-18). The mouth comes from `layout.dockOffset` (the
   station shipdata dock subentity position, `src/sim/world/layout.ts:150-165`), the depth from
   `DockGeometry.port.depth` (`src/content/generated/dock-port.json`, compiled from the dock subentity mesh by
   `tools/oolite-ingest/convert/dock-port.ts:249`, validated in `createDockGeometry`) and the axis from
   `layout.stationToPlanet`. Today that record is the Coriolis'; the per-station todo makes it per station and the
   launch view then follows with no change. A source guard keeps Coriolis literals out of the module.
4. Culling and winding. The dock's side walls face the slot interior (vendored
   `third_party/oolite/Models/oolite_coriolis_dock.dat`: 92 faces at x < 0 with +x normals, 90 at x > 0 with -x
   normals), and they are already seen from the slot interior when docking. `src/render/bodies/station.ts` sets no
   `side`, so the glTF default FrontSide stays: the walls draw from inside, and hull, faceplate and tris faces seen
   from inside the hull are back faces and are culled, so the opening shows space. No material change is planned.
5. Lighting. Unchanged: one directional sun and one ambient light, no shadows and no post-processing
   (`src/render/system-scene.ts:287-305`, calibrated in `src/render/exposure.ts`). Oolite draws the dock without
   self-shadowing too. The walls read with the dock texture and its authored emissive under that light; the
   opening shows the lit planet. The executor reports how dark the slot reads from the frames; a lighting change is
   out of scope.
6. Near plane and depth. `CAMERA_NEAR = 0.5` (`src/render/system-scene.ts:60`) with a logarithmic depth buffer
   (`src/app/boot.ts:56`). The cockpit sits 7.25 m off the axis and the slot is `port.width` 103.4 m by
   `port.height` 241.9 m, so the nearest wall is about 44 m away; a unit test asserts the lateral offset plus the
   near distance stays inside half the smaller slot dimension. The hull has `polygonOffset` against the dock
   (`station.ts:193-200`). Every body keeps `depthTest: true`, so the starfield, sun and planet appear only through
   the opening.
7. HUD: hidden for the launch, shown at handover, as today. Correction to the brief: Oolite does draw its HUD
   while launching (`Universe.m:4718-4732` renders it in every status except dead, escape and start-game;
   `HeadUpDisplay.m:1494` and `:2756` hide only the compass and ASC targets while launching), and it holds the roll
   at 0 (`PlayerEntity.m:6470`). Wegolite's D-10 reconciliation rolls the hull at the station rate during the exit
   (PROJECT.md "Launch roll is expressed in the station's world basis"), so showing the HUD now would show a roll
   bar and a compass blip that Oolite does not. Keeping it hidden is the smaller and safer change; Task 3 records
   Oolite's behaviour as a todo for the owner.
8. Station rotation. The hull rolls with the station during the exit (`launch.ts:58`, `:91-94`) and the camera
   follows the hull, so the slot walls hold still in the view while the planet and stars turn through the opening.
   That is what "the slot rotates with the station around the ship" looks like from a cockpit that rolls with the
   station. During launching `stationAngle` equals `stationRollRadPerSec * launchElapsed` (both reset in
   `beginLaunch`, both advanced per step), so the render rolls the station at the same interpolated time as the
   pose. Otherwise the walls would jitter against the camera by up to one step of roll.
9. Kept behaviours. Pause runs no step, so `launchElapsed` and the view freeze (the frame loop draws paused frames
   at alpha 0 and the redraw gate skips them). A skip after `launchSeen` makes the sim complete the launch, and the
   next frame is the in-flight cockpit (skip = jump to the end). The +600 s clock, `markSeen` at handover and the
   first full viewing are sim/event-router behaviour and do not change. There is no reduced-motion or accessibility
   setting (`rg -i 'reduced.?motion' src` finds nothing), so there is nothing to honour.
10. The overlay is used only by the launch (`rg` finds it only in `start-session.ts`, `context.ts`,
    `render-driver.ts` and its tests; docking and hyperspace do not use it), so it is deleted. Its phase timeline in
    `src/app/launch-sequence.ts` is dropped, and the module keeps only the skip gate (`canSkip`, `markSeen`,
    `reset`, `advance`). The external cinematic in `launch-camera.ts` has no other user and is deleted with its
    tests. The Cobra stays hidden. Its wings, 130 m across, would not fit the 103 m slot in the launch roll
    (PROJECT.md "Leave the Phase 3 launch orientation untouched"), but the view is a camera path, not a hull path,
    so nothing intersects on screen.
