# Quick task 261110-a3k: stop drawing unchanging frames

Executed inline by the main agent at the owner's request. Item P4 of
`.planning/reviews/2026-11-09-logic-security-review.md`. The owner allowed
recorded decisions to be revised where a better result justifies it.

## Why

`frame()` ended in `renderFrame()` on every animation frame. While docked the
fixed step runs but nothing in the scene advances, and while paused nothing
advances at all, so each frame re-shaded the same image, planet included,
behind the station screen or the 94 % opaque pause menu.

## Tasks

1. Add `src/app/redraw-gate.ts`: a pure gate that lets an idle frame be drawn
   `IDLE_SETTLE_DRAWS` times and then skipped, refilled by a simulating frame,
   an explicit request, or a change of identity in the keys the image depends
   on.
2. Wire it into `game-session.ts`: only the loop's own `renderFrame` call asks
   the gate; every direct call still draws. Skip only `renderer.render` and the
   HUD canvas draw, so the scene graph, hints and probe stay current. Request a
   redraw after the chart and save-screen branches, on `resize()` and from the
   `?test=1` taint callback.
3. Give `NebulaBody` a `revision()` so a texture that loads after the scene was
   drawn causes one more draw.
4. Publish `probe.frames` (loop frames) beside `probe.renders` (drawn frames).
5. Rewrite the three browser gates that relied on continued drawing:
   `engine.spec.ts` (cadence now sampled after launch; adds a proof that the
   docked loop is quiet), `budget.spec.ts` (settle on loop frames) and
   `hyperspace-failure.spec.ts` (the loop runs, the counters hold).
6. Unit tests for the gate and the session wiring; CHANGELOG and
   `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`.

## Verification

`typecheck`, `lint`, the unit suite, and the browser specs that read the draw
counters or change scene while idle: `engine`, `budget`,
`hyperspace-failure`, `resize`, `context-loss`, `throttle`, `settings`,
`dispose`, `aspect`, then the smoke subset.
