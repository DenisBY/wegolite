The acceptance thresholds (settled here; Claude's discretion per ROADMAP "Decisions to settle", from RESEARCH Open Question 2):
- Seeds: `ACCEPTANCE_EPOCHS` = epochs 1 to 20 of the Lave world (`worldSeedFor(marketSeedForSystem("g0-s7"), epoch)`), each populated with its 12 inert ships which the run converts to scripted flyers.
- Horizon: `HORIZON_TICKS` = 18,000 (150 simulated seconds), every tick checked.
- No stuck ship: for the station, intercept and flee scripts, while the goal is unmet (station and intercept: range above the script's desired range; flee: range below it), the goal metric must improve by at least `STUCK_MIN_PROGRESS_FRACTION` = 0.01 of its window-start value within every `STUCK_WINDOW_TICKS` = 3,600 window; `MAX_STUCK_WINDOWS` = 0. For every flyer, a window during which desiredSpeed stays at or above `FROZEN_MIN_DESIRED_SPEED` = 10 m/s must show at least `FROZEN_MIN_DISPLACEMENT_M` = 100 m of displacement; `MAX_FROZEN_WINDOWS` = 0. `MAX_FRUSTRATION_EVENTS` = 0 `shipAIFrustrated` events from station, intercept and flee flyers.
- No tunnelling: `MAX_HULL_CROSSINGS` = 0 tick segments of any ship's origin that hit another ship's or the player's octree hull by the two-stage rule (bounding sphere, then the octree line test); `MAX_STATION_SPHERE_ENTRIES` = 0 ticks with a ship origin inside the station's 500 m collision sphere; the player is parked (speed 0) outside both.
- No NaN repair: `MAX_REPAIRS` = 0 (sum of `entity.repairs`), `MAX_AI_FAULTED` = 0, `MAX_AI_DROPPED` = 0, and `hashWorld` never throws.
