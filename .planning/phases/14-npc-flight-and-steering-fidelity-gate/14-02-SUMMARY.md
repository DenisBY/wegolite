## Facts the later plans need

- **Behaviour quirks the port must keep.** A target exactly abeam (d_up exactly 0) gets no turn command from `trackPrimaryTarget` (no 0.01 fallback, unlike `trackDestination`). A target dead behind gives stick_pitch -max_flight_pitch * 0.125 (the pitch-over stick is overwritten), with `pitchingOver` set. At retreat, jink x and y are scaled by `range / 2000` for accuracy >= 6 (x3 more at >= 9) and z is not (9105). When `ATTACK_FLY_TO_TARGET` switches to ATTACK_TARGET at 4632 the rest of that call still runs.
- **`performFlee` draw order:** jink pair (two ranrot, none below accuracy 0), then one `randf()` only when accuracy > 0 and the approach aspect > 0.9995 (u < 0.15 EVASIVE_ACTION, else FLEE_EVASIVE_ACTION). The behaviour of those two rows is therefore checked from tick 1.
- **Frustration ticks, by fround accumulation:** 0.5 s at 61, 15 s at 1801 (also 7.5 s at 901, 9.375 s at 1126, 10 s at 1201, 12.5 s at 1501). The evasive follow-on is tick 61 after a reset at tick 0; FLEE_EVASIVE_ACTION's follow-on redraws the 400-jink pair and does not reset frustration.
- **Tracking curve:** aim point is the tick-0 position through tick 119, P0 + 1.5 v at tick 120 (v the target's velocity), v (s + 0.5)(s + 1) added to P0 at 121 with s = 0.5 + 1/120, and the exact position at 180. An `aimPoint` check at tick k is the curve evaluated at the integer clock k after k updates.
- **Runner requirements for plan 14-09:** a `self.targetId` that names no body (999) is a dead reference, so `primaryTarget` is nil (the planet row works the same way after `setTarget`); initial `self.behaviour` is injected without calling `perform`; `rangeToTarget` is checked only at tick 0; several rows have a nonzero initial `successFactor` or `destination` that the port must load verbatim.
- **The Shuttle's weapon range is 0**, so an unarmed attacker with zero jink alternates ATTACK_TARGET and ATTACK_FLY_FROM_TARGET every tick; jink.x = 200 stays in fly-from at range 1000 (threshold 15 x 200 = 3000), jink.x = -200 leaves at once. Plan 14-14's must_have has these rows to cite.
- **Proximity zone:** `PROXIMITY_WARN_DISTANCE2` is 16, so the pair trigger is four radii, not two. The range-0 and negative-range flee rows overlap it by necessity (range 0 means the centre distance is the radius sum); their alert is set at the end of tick 1 and read at tick 2, past their horizon of 1.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 3 - blocking] "A ship with `maxFlightSpeed` 0" is not expressible**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (flee frustration row)
- **Issue:** the contract's `stats.maxFlightSpeed` is strictly positive, so the plan's example pose cannot be written.
- **Fix:** the ship rests with `desiredSpeed` 0 and a `desiredRange` below the range, so flee takes the safe branch and never raises speed: the same static pose, proved by `STATIC_SELF` in the self-test. No contract change.
- **Commit:** `f847f17`

**2. [Rule 2 - correctness] Initial frustration values rounded differently in float32**
- **Found during:** Task 3 review of the accumulator contract
- **Issue:** 9.999, 2.999 and 10.4 are not float32-exact, so a port that rounds the fixture's initial value on load and one that does not would differ in the last bit of a tolerance-0 check.
- **Fix:** exact values 10 - 2^-10, 3 - 2^-10 and 10.375, plus a self-test assertion for every fixture.
- **Commit:** `36a1c60`

**3. Scope note (no code change).** ATTACK_FLY_TO_TARGET's accuracy >= 9 evasive dodge (4721-4729) is the one in-scope line no fixture covers; see key-decisions. Stock accuracy cannot reach it, and the fly-from twin (4790-4797) is covered by two rows.

**Total deviations:** 2 auto-fixed plus 1 scope note. **Impact:** none on the plan's IN list; the unreachable-from-stock branch is recorded for plan 14-14's ledger.
