Steering contract (binding for plans 14-08 to 14-16): `steerShip(entity, view, stats, dt, draw, emit)` where `view` is a `WorldView`, `stats` an `NpcFlightStats`, `draw()` returns the next unit draw from the actor's own `combat` stream (plan 14-09 builds it over `entity.rng.combat` with write-back, as `invoke` does for the `ai` stream; the runner passes the fixture's scripted draws), and `emit(event)` receives `{ name, a? }` with a name from `AI_EVENT_NAMES` (plan 14-09 posts it with `postAiEvent` to the entity's own AI). Order inside one call: (1) if `steer.accuracy` is the unresolved sentinel, draw it once as `u * 10 - 5`, negated for `CLASS_POLICE` and `CLASS_MILITARY`, through the `setAccuracy:` clamp (`ShipEntity.m:665-672, 1126-1143`; a lazy draw instead of Oolite's setup draw so the populator's world stream does not move, Pitfall 3); (2) if `steer.entryPending`, complete the pending perform entry and clear the flag; (3) the behaviour from the dispatch switch (`processBehaviour:`, `ShipEntity.m:2485-2638`); (4) `applyAttitudeChanges` then `applyThrust` when Oolite's flag for that behaviour is set. Steering has no try/catch (FOUND-15 containment is for AI modules only), so it must be total. Random draws obey the oracle contract rule: one unit draw per `randf()`, `floor(u * N)` for `ranrot_rand() % N`. Frame: Wegolite local forward is -Z, right +X, up +Y (`src/sim/combat/aim.ts` `LOCAL_FORWARD`); Oolite's world maps to Wegolite's by negating z, and the attitude composition's signs are whatever makes the oracle's attitude fixture pass (Pitfall 1: resolved by test, never by guessing).
