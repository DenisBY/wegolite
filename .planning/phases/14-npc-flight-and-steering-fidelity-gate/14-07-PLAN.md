- Horizon: `HORIZON_TICKS` = 18,000 (150 simulated seconds), every tick checked; tick t0 is the first tick after the conversion.
- Goal metrics (one definition each, used identically by the flight-script pilot's done check, the flyers scenario and the detectors): station script, the centre distance from the flyer to the station centre, goal met at or below the script range; intercept, `rangeToPrimaryTarget` to the player (centre distance minus both collision radii, `ShipEntity.m:9718-9733`), goal met below the script range; flee, the same range to the player, goal met above the script range.
- Windows: fixed, non-overlapping windows of `STUCK_WINDOW_TICKS` = 3,600 aligned to t0 (window k covers ticks t0 + 3,600k to t0 + 3,600(k + 1) − 1; five windows per seed). The window-start value is the metric at the window's first tick and the window-end value the metric at its last tick.
- No stuck ship: a window is evaluated for a station, intercept or flee flyer only when its goal is unmet at every tick of the window (a goal met at any tick, including mid-window, removes that window, and every later window of that flyer, from evaluation). Progress is start − end for station and intercept and end − start for flee (net change of the goal metric, not path length). A window is stuck when progress is below `STUCK_MIN_PROGRESS_FRACTION` = 0.01 × |start| or below `STUCK_MIN_PROGRESS_M` = 1 m; `MAX_STUCK_WINDOWS` = 0. `MAX_FRUSTRATION_EVENTS` = 0 `shipAIFrustrated` events delivered to station, intercept and flee flyers while their goal is unmet.
- No frozen ship: a window is evaluated for every flyer whose `nav.desiredSpeed` is at or above `FROZEN_MIN_DESIRED_SPEED` = 10 m/s at every tick of the window; it is frozen when the path length (the sum of the per-tick origin displacements, not the net displacement) over the window is below `FROZEN_MIN_DISPLACEMENT_M` = 100 m; `MAX_FROZEN_WINDOWS` = 0. (Orbiting is the stuck detector's job; the frozen detector catches a ship told to move that does not.)
- No tunnelling: `MAX_HULL_CROSSINGS` = 0 tick segments (a flyer's origin at tick k − 1 to its origin at tick k) that hit another ship's or the player's octree hull by the two-stage rule (that body's bounding sphere, then the octree line test), every other ship and the player being candidates; `MAX_STATION_SPHERE_ENTRIES` = 0 ticks with a flyer origin strictly inside the station's collision sphere, whose radius is `STATION_RADIUS_M` = 500, restated from `layout.station.radius` (built from the unexported `STATION_RADIUS` at `src/sim/world/layout.ts:71`; plan 14-17 asserts the two agree).
- Hull-crossing classes and their pre-declared resolution (fixed now, before the port and before the thresholds are frozen; Addresses checker warning: a faithful port may breach the zero-crossing criterion): every crossing segment is classified, and both classes count toward `MAX_HULL_CROSSINGS`, which stays 0, the literal reading of "no tunnelling", never weakened silently. `HULL_CROSSING_CLASSES` = `own-target` and `other`. A crossing is `own-target` when, at tick k − 1, the crossing flyer's `nav.targetId` is the body whose hull it crosses; every other crossing is `other`. Why the class exists: Oolite's in-scope target behaviours read the proximity alert through `hasProximityAlertIgnoringTarget:YES` (intercept `ShipEntity.m:3881`, slow dogfight 3950, attack fly-to 4631, attack fly-from 4787, flee 4898; the predicate at 9751-9757), so an alert raised by their own target never turns them away, and a faithful attacker or interceptor can pass through the parked player. Pre-declared resolution: an `other` crossing is treated as a port defect and fixed in `src/sim/npc/` along the cited lines with every oracle green; if the port matches its oracles and an `other` crossing remains, plan 14-17 stops at its budgeted checkpoint with option (c) below recommended. An `own-target` crossing by a port whose every oracle test passes is classified now as Oolite's own behaviour reproduced, not a port defect; plan 14-17 stops at its budgeted checkpoint and offers the owner exactly these options, with (a) recommended: (a) accept the listed `own-target` crossings as a recorded divergence of the acceptance criterion (a clause in plan 14-19's grouped Key Decisions row on the acceptance thresholds, the run recorded as passing every other threshold with the `own-target` crossings listed per seed, `thresholds.ts` unchanged so every later run reports them the same way); (b) revise the criterion by an owner decision recorded in PROJECT.md (for example, gate only `other` crossings), followed by a `thresholds.ts` commit that names the decision; (c) treat the crossings as a port defect and investigate further. The executor never chooses among these itself.
- Player parking rule (deterministic, fixed before the run): the player is parked at speed 0 with the identity orientation at the first candidate `station.position + PLAYER_PARK_DISTANCE_M × a` for `a` in the order +x, −x, +y, −y, +z, −z of the world frame, with `PLAYER_PARK_DISTANCE_M` = 6,000 (5,500 m outside the station sphere), that lies at least `PLAYER_PARK_MIN_SHIP_CLEARANCE_M` = 2,000 from every ship's start position and at least `PLAYER_PARK_MIN_PLANET_CLEARANCE_M` = 1,000 outside the planet's radius; a seed with no qualifying candidate fails, it is never skipped.
