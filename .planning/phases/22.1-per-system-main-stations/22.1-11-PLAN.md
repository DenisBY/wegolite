## Decisions this plan implements

- **O3 for the models** (planner's choice, revised in review round 1): per-kind lazy GLB; the current kind awaited at the async entry points; the destination prefetched at the countdown start; a still-loading model attaches late, never a wrong model and never a throw. The background fetch of every kind after the first in-flight frame is dropped (review HIGH, consensus concern 1; the reviewers' preferred fix): it would race the first-load measurements of `budget.spec.ts` and `traffic.spec.ts`, cost about 10 MB per player and three decoded texture sets in memory, and it buys only the first arrival at an unvisited kind on a slow link, which the countdown prefetch and the late attach already cover. Reversibility: reversible (adding it back would need both specs amended with fetch stamps and an owner decision on what the ceiling measures).
- **Owner decision D-C** (2026-11-27, binding; recorded in PROJECT.md by plan 22.1-01, an owner decision and not a planner choice; research O4): station flashers are deferred out of Phase 22.1 for every station, because they are absent today for every station, the Coriolis included, and so unrelated to the station kind. This plan carries the deferral: the todo, a test assertion that names it, and a FIDELITY.md divergence row (Oolite draws them; Wegolite never has) quoting the D-C flashers sentence already in PROJECT.md. Reversibility: reversible.
- **Pitfall P5**: node lists from the record.
