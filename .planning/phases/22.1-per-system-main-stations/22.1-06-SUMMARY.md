# Phase 22.1 Plan 06: The station's laser target and ident through the layout, and per-kind docking Summary

**The main station's static row now carries no Coriolis key: `staticHullKey` and `staticIdentKey` resolve it through `layout.stationKey`, so a laser shot and an ident lock at Biarge and Zaonce test and name the Dodecahedron and Icosahedron, and docking, the player's hull contact, the launch twist and the hand-over are proven through each kind's own dock record.**

## Accomplishments

- `statics.ts`: `StaticTarget = StationStaticTarget | BuoyStaticTarget`; the station row is `{ id, layoutKey: "station", healthKey }`, the buoy rows keep `hullKey` and `identKey`. `staticHullKey` and `staticIdentKey` take `(row, layout)`. `STATIC_TARGETS` stays one `Object.freeze`d module-level table (Pitfall P7, T-22.1-14); the module header now says the station's octrees are those of the system's own station.
- Consumers: `step.ts` resolves the hull lookup and the `HitTarget.specKey` through `staticHullKey` (no hull in the table: no candidate, as before); `ident.ts` emits `identLocked` through `staticIdentKey`; `death.ts` narrows on `layoutKey` (below); `kill.ts` and `collide.ts` compile unchanged (their station paths already return or `continue` before reading a key); `ledger.ts` is byte-identical to the base (it reads `layoutKey` and `healthKey` only).
- **Death-cause consequence (stated, not hidden).** `playerDeathCause` can no longer name the station as a culprit, because its row has no key: a station-caused death yields `{ damage }` with no culprit. This cannot happen today: the station neither shoots nor collides (`collide.ts` skips its row, FIDELITY E-135), and the new comment in `death.ts` says so. The E-135 todo lists `src/sim/combat/death.ts` in its `files` and a short "Death cause (Phase 22.1)" paragraph, so the follow-up phase for E-135 threads the layout there when the station becomes a collider.
- Per-kind laser, ident: a shot aimed at each station's centre from behind it hits `MAIN_STATION_ID` with a hull table holding only that kind's octree and ends on the near shell (short of the centre by less than the collision radius). With the traffic-on table that holds only the Coriolis hull (a Biarge or Zaonce session before plan 22.1-09 merges the artifacts), or with another kind's artifact, the station is no candidate and the beam runs past its centre. Ident at Zaonce emits `identLocked` `icosahedron-station` and frames the station at its own radius.
- Per-kind dock, hull contact and twist (details below).

## Per-kind dock, twist and hand-over results

The aligned roll is derived from each record's port width and height (portrait slot: wings along the tall axis; landscape: along the wide axis), never a typed angle per kind.

| Kind | Slot (record) | `slotTwistedForShip` | Aligned approach | Quarter turn | Part past the mouth (`contactPort.zMax`) | Hand-over `box.min.z` beyond it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coriolis (Lave) | portrait 103.4 x 241.9 | true | docks on the weighted-plane tick | bounces | about 34.0 m | yes, in the lane, no scrape, bounce or dock |
| Dodecahedron (Biarge) | landscape 210.4 x 81.0 | false | docks | bounces | about 25.7 m | yes |
| Icosahedron (Zaonce) | portrait 82.1 x 238.5 | true | docks | bounces | about 5.2 m | yes |

- Launch: wings along the slot's long axis for every kind (twisted launch is the stationBaseQuat quarter roll for the Coriolis and Icosahedron; the Dodecahedron launches with `stationBaseQuat` itself, no quarter roll); each leaves exactly at `layout.dockOffset` from its station centre.
- Hull contact per kind (D-B): a ship box overlapping that kind's `contactPort` outside the aperture gets a hull contact (unit normal, positive depth) and a `bounce` with cause `hull` from the dock step; the same box one metre past `contactPort.zMax` gets none (`hullContact` and `obbsIntersect` null/false, no event). The Dodecahedron's faceplate protrusion (`zMax` about 25.7 m) is inside its contact box: a box touching it is a hull bounce, not a dock. The proxies come from the three dock records. These tests prove the proxy is used, not that it fits the visible hull; the E-135 todo paragraph "Contact proxy per station kind (Phase 22.1)" records what the box costs per kind.
- Mutation check: forcing the aligned roll to 0 for every kind fails the Dodecahedron docking case (the test can fail).
