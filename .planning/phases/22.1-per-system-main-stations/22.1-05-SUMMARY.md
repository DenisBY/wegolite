# Phase 22.1 Plan 05: The station's own mass feeds the witchspace blockage Summary

**Every system's main station now blocks a completing witchspace jump with the compiled mass its layout carries (Coriolis 7,179.7 m, Dodecahedron 9,551.7 m, Icosahedron 9,090.2 m), through a required `StepOptionsInput.stationMass`; the Coriolis mass literal and the kind-to-mass function are gone and no Coriolis hash moved.**

## Accomplishments

- `StepOptionsInput.stationMass: number` is required and `buildStepOptions` always sets `hyperspace.stationMass`; the system-kind derivation and its import are removed. The sim's own `StepOptions.hyperspace.stationMass` stays optional (the frozen `tests/acceptance/detectors.ts` builds hyperspace options without it); the doc comment in `step.ts` now says the app always supplies it.

- `countdown.ts`: `CORIOLIS_STATION_MASS` and `witchspaceStationMass` deleted; the header and blocker-list comments say the mass is the system's own station record. `isWitchspaceBlocked`, `WITCH_BLOCK_K` and the buoy masses are unchanged.
- Callers changed: `sim-driver.ts`, `tests/helpers/tick-script.ts`, `tests/app/session/traffic-variants.test.ts`, `tests/sim/combat/collision-traffic.scenario.test.ts`, `tests/app/session/step-options.test.ts`, `tests/app/session/sim-driver.test.ts` (pass the layout's mass); `tests/sim/world/ship-field.test.ts`, `tests/sim/world/world-cadence.test.ts`, `tests/helpers/recorded-flights.ts` (read the layout's `stationMass` of the system each flies, all Coriolis, so no hash moved).
- Per-kind blockage radii (layout mass in parentheses): Coriolis at Lave 7,179.72 m (515,483,904), Dodecahedron at Biarge 9,551.74 m (912,357,312), Icosahedron at Zaonce 9,090.18 m (826,313,024). - Row 38 and row 40 of FIDELITY.md updated; the constants ledger gains the two new masses; the worked numbers list all three stations (measured in plan 22.1-01).
