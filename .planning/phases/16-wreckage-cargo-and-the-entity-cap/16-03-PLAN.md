## Phase 16 decisions this plan implements

- **D16-07 (one plate look, the cheaper option):** the plate is the `alloy` entry only; `scarred-alloy` (same mesh, a second 153,629-byte diffuse) is not compiled and the plate role draw is not taken (plan 16-04). Why the cheaper option: one fewer vendored texture, one fewer GLB and draw call, no extra code path, and Oolite's 50/50 role mix (research A2, assumed equal weights) is cosmetic. Reversible: adding the variant later is one catalogue row, one GLB and one draw.
- **D16-16 (wreck set and order):** the five wreck entries in shipdata order; plan 16-04 draws the model uniformly over this list (research A2).
- **Legacy texture rule (this plan):** an entry listed in `LEGACY_TEXTURED_ENTRIES` (the five wreck keys, each mapped to its legacy texture list, `["wreck.png"]`) may name a `.png` material key that its shipdata entry does not author; `materialFor` then projects an empty material dictionary (Oolite's default material: the texture as the diffuse, gloss 0, no normal, specular or emission), and the texture closure adds the listed names as diffuse references. Any other missing material still fails the build (D-21 of Phase 2 unchanged for every other entry).
