## Phase 16 decisions this plan implements

- **D16-01 (OC-1, narrows 15-CONTEXT D-06):** no commodity draw at the explosion. Every pod is one anonymous cargopod with the shipdata key `barrel` (`oolite_template_barrel`, `shipdata.plist:603-634`): no `getRandomAmountOfCommodity` amount, no 50 % commodity-role roll (`Universe.m:3863-3878`). A later scooping phase (CBT-11) adds the commodity with its own draw and accepts the changed stream (pre-release, nothing persists a world).
- **D16-24:** draws for features not modelled are not taken: the commodity, the amount, the pod role roll and `randomEjectaTemperature` here; the plate role draw and the big-ship ring roll in plan 16-04.
- **D16-12 (spawn and draw order, first part):** one local copy of `victim.rng.combat`, written back at the end: (1) the `canFragment` roll, (2) the pods in capsule index order, each survivor's 13 draws (box x, y, z; jitter x, y, z; velocity x, y, z; quaternion w, x, y, z) taken before its spawn is attempted. Plan 16-04 appends the wreckage, the plate and the blast after the pods, so no pod value moves. Rated costly below: changing the order re-pins every debris test and moves every debris hash, but no format or save changes.
- **D16-13:** `canFragment` is rolled at the explosion as the first draw (`u < fragmentChance`), not at ship setup as Oolite does (`ShipEntity.m:372`): no new hashed field, statistically equal.
- **D16-05 (the cap, pods part):** a piece spawns only while `liveEntityCount(world) < DEBRIS_ENTITY_LIMIT` (`0.95 * MAX_ENTITIES`, 243.2, so at most 243 live before the spawn); wreckage additionally below `WRECKAGE_ENTITY_LIMIT` (`0.5 * MAX_ENTITIES`, 128; used by 16-04). Tested before each piece, after its draws (divergence: Oolite tests once per explosion and still adds one plate above 0.95, `ShipEntity.m:8077-8080`). Dropped terms: the frame-time term `[UNIVERSE getTimeDelta] < 0.125` and the `isVisible || within scanner range` test (always true in a Wegolite world, research A9). Counting differences: Wegolite counts only live dynamic entities, the victim is already dead and render effects are not entities.
- **D16-06:** no eviction: pods and plates never expire (Oolite `dumbAI.plist` only pauses), a full world only sheds new debris, and a launch or jump replaces the world.
- **D16-15 (debris records):** pods are kind `cargo`, scan class `CLASS_CARGO`, `maxEnergy` 1 (`max_energy = 1`, `shipdata.plist:625`), `energyRechargeRate` 0, `ai` null, speed 0, radius the pod model's radius.
- **D16-18:** the box offset is added unrotated, as Oolite does, after `toWegoliteLocal` (the box is in Oolite's model axes; the jitter is symmetric and added per world axis).
- **D16-19:** the drift pass integrates `velocity` for kinds `cargo` and `wreckage` only; ships keep an unintegrated zero velocity until Phase 18 decides rebounds.
- **D16-03 (OC-3):** debris is never a hit, ident, scanner, mass-lock or jump-blockage candidate in this phase; the existing filters (`kind === "ship"` in the hit candidates, ident and `npcContacts`; `CLASS_CARGO` in `NON_LOCKING_SCAN_CLASSES`; no `shipMasses` row) already hold and are asserted here, not changed.
