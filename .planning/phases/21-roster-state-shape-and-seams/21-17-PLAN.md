Design rules for the atlas (the executor may refine the packing, never the guarantees):
- One layout per hull, shared by every slot, on UV channel 0: one square region per distinct tuple of (slot sources and baked values) among the hull's primitives, so two primitives that share a diffuse map but differ in gloss or emission get two regions; the region side is the largest native size in its tuple, and a smaller map in the tuple is upscaled by its integer ratio with nearest-neighbour replication (all sizes are powers of two), so nearest sampling stays texel-exact; maps are never shrunk.
- The atlas is square, so every square region has the same UV scale in u and v (validity condition (b)); regions are placed on a power-of-two grid with an edge-extended gutter so mip levels do not bleed across regions at the distances the regions' own mips cover; packing order is fixed (tuple order of first appearance in the authored material order), so the layout is deterministic.
- Roughness: when every primitive of the hull has the same roughness, the merged material keeps it as `roughnessFactor`; otherwise a metallic-roughness atlas (G = roughness, B = 0) in the same layout carries it and `roughnessFactor` is 1.
- The merged material keeps `KHR_materials_specular` when any primitive had a specular map, with white regions for the others (equal to the extension's defaults); the emissive slot is the baked mask per region (black where a primitive emits nothing); every primitive's `extras.oolite` is kept, in order, under the merged material's `extras.oolite.merged`.
- Atlas PNGs are encoded deterministically (`node:zlib`, as `encodeGreyscalePng` does, widened to RGBA) and re-encoded to WebP by the existing encoder with the profile of the slot's map kind; the emissive slot stays a greyscale PNG, as the emissive bake ships today; the encodings row lists every vendored input of the atlas.
