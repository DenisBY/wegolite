  - Selling is the mirror of buying: `feasible = min(held, 127 - stock)`, never below 0; proceeds are `price * amount`; stock rises and price is unchanged.
  - At stock 127 a sell refuses with `marketFull`; at held 0 it refuses with `nothingToSell`; a request above the feasible amount is clamped and reports the limiting reason.
  - Bulk buy limit is `min(stock, floor(credits/price), availablePods)`; kg/g limit is `min(stock, floor(credits/price), availablePods * podSize + safeMax - alreadyHeld)` with a floor of 0.

  - `applyBuy`/`applySell` re-run the plan, refuse on any amount mismatch or refusal, and return the original objects unchanged.
