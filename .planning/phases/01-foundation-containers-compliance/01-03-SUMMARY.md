## Decisions Made

- **Tick through `canon`.** `serialize` passes the tick through `canon(tick, 0)`; for these integer ticks the text is identical to a raw `${tick}`, so the plan's "every number through canon" rule holds without changing the hash.
- **Velocity from the forward vector.** Thrust accelerates a scalar speed that is then applied along the rotated forward vector, which exercises all four `PROBE_*` placeholders while keeping `step()` a pure function of `(state, input, dt)`.
- **`toBe` on the timestep.** The fixture's `dt` is asserted against the imported `DT`, so a decimal transcription error fails the replay test rather than silently falsifying what the fixture records.
