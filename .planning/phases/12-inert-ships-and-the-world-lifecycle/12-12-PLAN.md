<objective>
Make a dock or a jump that completes behind the galactic chart close it, and make the game loop safe against the `reset()` that `closeChart` performs from inside a step (RESEARCH Pattern 6, Pitfall 7). These are the consequences of WORLD-11; plan 12-13 then lets the simulation run behind the chart in flight.

Purpose: once the chart no longer freezes flight, a dock or an arrival can complete while it is open; without these rules the station shell or the destination would appear under an open chart with the wrong input context, and the loop reset inside the step would leave a negative accumulator.

Output: `StationFlowDeps.closeChart`, `HyperspaceControllerDeps.closeChart`, the facade wiring, the game-loop reset guard and its new test file.
</objective>
