<objective>
Compile the NPC ship records the inert world uses (DATA-10 records, DATA-11 stage 1 as a staged check) into a new generated file, `src/content/generated/ships-npc.json`, behind a strict schema, a hand-written golden and the drift gate, and give the client a lazy loader for it (DATA-13).

Purpose: Phase 12's populator picks ships by role and needs, per ship, its roles, AI name, scan class and model, plus the URL of the model it will fetch on demand. Phase 10 adds the mass to the same record. `ships-client.json` stays untouched: its entries are byte-for-byte catalogue copies the first load imports (owner decision; RESEARCH "Alternatives Considered").

D-02, per record (the canonical, non-template record of each compiled model is kept; every other record that uses one of the nine models is left out):

| Record(s) | Their roles | Decision and reason |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| adder, cobramk1, gecko, krait, mamba, moray, shuttle, sidewinder, viper | the populator roles listed in plan 09-03 | kept: one canonical record per model, together covering police, trader, pirate, hunter, shuttle and scavenger |
| adder-player, cobramk1-player, moray-player, morayMED-player | player | left out: player ships, not NPCs |
| cobramk1-alt | pirate(0.25), pirate-light-freighter(0.5), escort-heavy(0.5), escort-medium(0.25) | left out: pirate is already carried by seven kept records; the set is deliberately not probability-faithful (D-01) and its materials differ from the cobramk1 GLB's owner |
| morayMED | trader(0.5), sunskim-trader | left out: trader is already carried by adder, cobramk1 and moray; sunskim-trader is not a first-populator role |
| cobramk1-miner | miner | left out: mining is not in this milestone |
| mamba-escort | pirate-light-fighter(0.5), escort, escort-medium(0.25) | left out: escort roles are not placed by the first populator |
| viper-pursuit | wingman | left out: not a populator role |
| oolite-tutorial-krait, oolite-tutorial-krait-wave1, oolite_template_tutorial-krait-wave1 | tutorial roles | left out: tutorial only |
| every oolite_template_* record of the nine models | (template) | left out as records; each is the like_ship parent that the kept record resolves to |

Record shape (Claude's discretion in CONTEXT, resolved per RESEARCH Pattern 3): `key`, `roles`, `aiType`, `scanClass`, `model`, `glb`. `roles` stays verbatim so it equals the catalogue (Phase 12 parses `name(probability)` when it needs weights). `glb` is a locator, not inert-world data; it exists so the model to fetch has one drift-gated source. The compiled `aiType` is the authored `ai_type`; Oolite replaces it at spawn from `autoAImap.plist` when `auto_ai` is on, which is Phase 12's concern, not compiled here.

Output: the schema, the compiler and emitter, the generated file, the hand-written golden, the lazy client loader and their tests, and the NOTICE paragraph.
</objective>
