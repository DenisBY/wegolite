## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-01:** the simulation is frozen while dead; only the presentation clock moves.
- **D-06:** no auto-restart timer (Oolite's 30 s `kDeadResetTime` is not ported; FIDELITY row in 20-10).
- **D-07:** Esc, the F-keys, pause and every flight binding are ignored; no pause overlay ever opens over the game-over view.
- **D-08:** the chart cannot be (re)opened over the game-over view (20-04 closes one that was open at death).
