- DOCK-03 and DOCK-06 are unclassified by the specless edge probe. Flagged assumptions, each turned into an acceptance criterion: (a) zero-speed brush: the response guarantees outward separation motion, not merely a position push; (b) ship centre inside the box: a one-tick push-out along the minimum-translation axis; (c) high speed: normal flight is capped at 350 u/s (2.9 m/tick) and torus at 358,400 u/s, so the SAT is evaluated against the actual post-integration pose rather than a swept volume, and the sanitize clamp bounds the worst case.
- Collision proxy (explicit product choice): contact uses the emitted `contact` box, a conservative axis-aligned proxy covering hull + tris + faceplate. It includes empty volume around the diamond hull, so contact can fire slightly before visible contact; it never misses the protruding faceplate. Recorded in PROJECT.md. The proxy is consumed only through `DockGeometry.contactPort`, converted once by 04-02 from station-local to port coordinates (`zMin ≈ -1000.2561`, `zMax ≈ +34.0419`); this module never re-projects it.
- Nose gate and the proxy's forward face: band correction and straddle classification require port-frame `box.min.z < 0` strictly (DockEntity.m:727), so the wings-across launch pose is never a straddle. Because the conservative proxy reaches port z +34.0419 (the faceplate), a non-lane ship approaching from the front contacts the proxy before its nose crosses the plane; the band-correction path is reached only with the nose already inside, exactly as D-03 words it, and its tests place the ship there with `placeShipInPortFrame`. Accepted consequence of the box proxy, not a new decision.
- Tuning constants (feel only, discretionary): restitution 0.5, speed damping 0.35, impulse decay 1/(1+dt/0.6), impulse floor 8 m/s, shake amplitude 0.6 m / 0.35 s, flash 0.40 s. All within D-08's 0.3-0.5 s.
- Lane and band outcomes suppress hull contact for the tick (the tunnel interior is not collidable inside the footprint); outside and straddle are collidable, but straddle takes priority so a single tick never produces two responses.
- All bounce decisions here are reversible (D-07 shape; constants discretionary).
