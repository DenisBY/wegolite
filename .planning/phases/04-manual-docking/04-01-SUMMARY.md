## Decisions Made

- The single schema is the contract in both directions: the emitter cannot write a record the loader rejects, and the loader cannot accept one the emitter could not write.
- `source` names the vendored snapshot (`third_party/oolite`); every measured number is traceable to that pin.
- Zero-width apertures fail in the expansion loop ("no progress") before the schema sees them, which is the desired shape: the hang becomes a named `SCHEMA` failure.
