<objective>
Close the two remaining determinism holes: ban the APIs that are spec-permitted to differ between engines or architectures from the generator's own lint zone, and prove the whole emitted tree is byte-identical when regenerated under arm64 and amd64 from the same pinned image digest.

Purpose: byte determinism is a discipline, not a library feature. Almost everything in this pipeline is architecture-independent because it uses `+ - * /`, `Math.sqrt`, integers, and SHA-256 — but only while nothing reintroduces `Math.sin`, `Intl`, or `toFixed`, and only while the zlib-compressed emissive PNG is measured rather than assumed. This plan makes both claims falsifiable and records the result.

Output: an ESLint tools zone with a negative proof, `scripts/check-arch-determinism.sh` with an explicitly empty exclusion list, and the recorded outcome of its first run.
</objective>
