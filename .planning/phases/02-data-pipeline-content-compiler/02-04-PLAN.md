<objective>
Parse the five pinned `.dat` meshes into a typed, validated in-memory representation — the Cobra Mk III and the four-part Coriolis composite — including the comma-separated Coriolis dialect, the three normal modes, Oolite's winding repair, and bounding-box validation.

Purpose: DATA-03 is the last string-parsing dependency of the phase and the input to every GLB byte. The measured traps here — commas as separators, zero Cobra face normals, a per-face TEXTURES section, the explicit `NORMALS` block overriding `smooth` — are invisible in review and catastrophic in output. This plan settles them against the real meshes before a writer exists.

Output: `tools/oolite-ingest/convert/dat.ts`, five fixtures, `dat.test.ts`, the five vendored models with provenance, and exact count/bounding-box assertions. No GLB bytes yet.
