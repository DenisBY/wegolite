<objective>
Turn the three catalogue plists into typed, drift-checked content: all 2,048 systems across eight galaxies, all 41 equipment entries with their condition metadata preserved, and the six `descriptions.plist` label arrays plus the `sysdata-*` format templates.

Purpose: these are the numbers every later phase reads — Phase 3 places Lave from the system record, Phase 5 prices from the commodity and equipment records, Phase 6 plots the chart from the system chunks. Getting the stable ID, the raw-value rule, the unit rule, and the absent-vs-present rule right here is what keeps a fidelity port from becoming a plausibility port.

Output: `systems/g0.json`…`g7.json`, `equipment.json`, `labels.json`, three zod schema modules, the D-14 loader (`src/content/systems.ts`), two golden fixtures, and an updated manifest. No market generation, no condition-script evaluation, no UI.
</objective>
