<objective>
Vendor exactly the textures the two converted models reference, copy them byte-for-byte, and bake a separate greyscale emissive PNG from the source alpha channel — with a small PNG decoder and encoder built on `node:zlib` rather than an image dependency.

Purpose: DATA-07's material side is where silent substitution is most tempting (a missing texture, a white fallback) and where the documented Oolite pitfall lives (assigning the RGBA diffuse as an emissive map makes the whole Coriolis hull glow). The bake also produces the phase's one genuinely architecture-dependent output, so its determinism is proven here and again across architectures in plan 08.

Output: `tools/oolite-ingest/convert/textures.ts`, the referenced PNGs copied verbatim into `public/textures/`, the baked emissive map(s), an 8×8 asymmetric fixture, and the manifest and provenance rows.
</objective>
