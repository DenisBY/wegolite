## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-01:** collisions are decided octree against octree, Oolite's own heuristic (its crude prunes make the answer a subset of exact geometry; fidelity means the same heuristic).
- **PD-01 (contact half):** a detached buoy subentity is dropped from contact tests; `doOctreesCollide` takes an optional set of detached subentity indices.
- **Numeric choice:** float64 like `isHitByLine` (grazing cases can differ from Oolite's float32; fixtures are cell-centred).
