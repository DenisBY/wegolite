## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-01, PD-02:** the full `collideWithShip` response and damage from the right half; the buoy is an ordinary mover (only Oolite's station is special, and the station is not a collider in Wegolite).
- **PD-01 (collision half):** a contact on the buoy's frangible subentity uses the subentity's mass and puts dam2 on the subentity, while the response moves the parent buoy (11057-11070, 11103-11105, 11123-11128).
- **PD-05:** `canScoop` (11022-11027) is out of phase: no fixture models scooping; rammed pods take damage.
- **D-02:** braking, the player by raw `thrust` with the cleanup terms, a ship (and a buoy) by `SHIP_THRUST_FACTOR x thrust`.
- **D-03:** the blast on ships and the player. Configurations the fixtures cover for D-03's bounds: the victim's and the receiver's spheres just touching (|p| = r_victim + r_receiver) for the largest NPC victim against the lightest NPC receiver and against the player, and the floor (the receiver's centre within its own radius of the victim's centre). - **Fixture conventions:** Oolite's frame, metres, seconds; ids kebab-case, unique across every oracle tree, prefixed `collide-`, `brake-`, `scrape-` or `blast-`; every fixture has `citations` (file:line) and `derivation`.
