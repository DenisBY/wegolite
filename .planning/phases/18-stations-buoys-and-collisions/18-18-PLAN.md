## Decisions this plan implements

- **PD-02 (collision half):** buoys move when rammed; the response applies to the buoy side (Oolite carries a buoy about 10 m after a 100 m/s ram, about 122 m after 350 m/s). Supersedes the draft "buoys are immovable".
- **PD-01 (collision half):** a contact on the frangible subentity damages the subentity (11123-11128) and moves the parent; a detached subentity is gone from contact; the buoy's mass drops by the subentity's.
- **Planner divergence, recorded:** the C's position nudge of a contacted subentity moves the subentity inside its parent (it sets the subentity's own position, 11143-11146); Wegolite keeps the subentity at its authored offset.
- **Tripwire rule (owner, 2026-11-18):** the deferred player reversal is a todo plus a guard that fails on its first consumer (Phase 20's death path).
