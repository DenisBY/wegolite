## Decisions this plan implements

- **PD-06 (owner, 2026-11-19, after the cross-AI review):** buoys join the NPC proximity pass as in Oolite (`CollisionRegion.m:308-311`, `ShipEntity.m:6107`), amending the Phase 14 participant list; worlds with an NPC near a buoy may hash differently, and the QUAL-11 acceptance run is rerun after the change.
- **Planner choice (recorded):** the buoy body comes from the world view, which now reports a buoy at its live pose and omits a destroyed one, so the AI's other readers of the view (scans, target validity) see the same buoy the pass sees; with a zero offset the view's numbers are unchanged.
- **PD-08 (owner, 2026-11-19, after review round 2):** port Oolite's collision-chain prefilter (`Universe.m:6713-6780` and its y and x passes) for the buoy proximity alert, at least for pairs that include a buoy, and for every pair only if that keeps the Phase 14 pins; without it Wegolite alerts at about 236.6 m where Oolite alerts at 164 to 219 m, and PD-07 would then accept less faithful numbers. Phase 14's recorded reason for omitting it ("never affects a pair of equal radii; a no-op near the station, where the planet chains every entity") does not hold for buoys: the radii differ for every NPC kind, and the witchpoint beacon is over 400 km from the planet, outside its chain.
- **PD-07 (owner, 2026-11-19), the QUAL-11 rule for this plan:** with buoys in the proximity pass, NPCs in the frozen acceptance worlds may steer around the witchpoint beacon, so measured values can move; that is the intended behaviour. A rerun that passes 21 of 21 under the unchanged thresholds and detectors is accepted with its moved values, and the SUMMARY records old and new numbers; no `Detector-change-approved-by-owner` trailer is used, because no threshold or detector changes. Only a seed that fails a threshold stops the plan: it is reported to the owner and investigated as a possible bug.
