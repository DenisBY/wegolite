## Decisions this plan implements

- **Carried forward:** only buoys hold damage, in `World.staticHealth`; the station is never destroyed.
- **D-04 (sim half):** a destroyed buoy stays destroyed for the life of its world; a new world restores it.
- **D-05 (first half):** a destroyed buoy stops being a target; ident, blockage, compass, scanner and view follow in 18-06 to 18-08, all reading `isBuoyDestroyed`.
- **PD-02 (pose half):** the buoy's position is its layout position plus its per-world offset.
- **Planner decision (numbers checked):** a destroyed buoy releases no debris (Oolite's `n_alloys = floor(sqrt(sqrt(698,691 / 25,000))) = 2` would give 0 to 3 wrecks and 0 or 1 plate; the debris draws come from a per-entity stream that statics do not have; recorded in FIDELITY); its blast is plan 18-19's.
