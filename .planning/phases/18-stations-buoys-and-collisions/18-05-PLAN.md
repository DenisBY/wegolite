## Decisions this plan implements

- **Carried forward (CONTEXT):** only buoys hold damage, in `World.staticHealth` (Phase 15, D-08/D-09); the station is never destroyed.
- **D-04 (sim half):** a destroyed buoy stays destroyed for the life of its `World`; a new world brings it back (`createStaticHealth()`); saves are unchanged.
- **D-05 (first half):** a destroyed buoy stops being a target; its removal from the view, scanner, ident lock, compass and blockage is plans 18-06 to 18-08, all reading `isBuoyDestroyed`.
- **D-07:** pods and plates are shot and destroyed at zero energy; wrecks are not candidates.
- **Planner decisions (numbers verified 2026-11-19, the owner's "verify negligible claims with numbers" rule):** the buoy subentity's own energy is not tracked (199 of 20,000 aimed beams end on it; killing it takes 14 such hits while the buoy dies at 17); a destroyed buoy releases no debris (Oolite's `n_alloys = floor(sqrt(sqrt(698,691 / 25,000))) = 2` would give 0 to 3 wrecks and 0 or 1 plate; statics have no per-entity stream, and adding one is a snapshot amendment); a destroyed pod or plate leaves silently. Each is a FIDELITY row and is listed for the owner in the SUMMARY. The blast from a destroyed buoy, pod or plate is plan 18-12's.
