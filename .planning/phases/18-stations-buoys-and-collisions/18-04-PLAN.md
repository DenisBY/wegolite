## Decisions this plan implements

- **Carried forward (CONTEXT):** statics are hit by porting Oolite's subentity rule; the station only absorbs hits and is never destroyed; Phase 18 supersedes "Statics are neither laser nor ident targets" in the same commit as the rule change (this plan supersedes the laser half; plan 18-06 completes the ident half on the same new row).
- **D-07 (station half):** laser hits require Oolite's `canCollide` (`Universe.m:5578`); the station can collide (`STATUS_ACTIVE`).
- **A6 (research assumption, recorded here):** the station's energy is not decremented. Oolite's `StationEntity` lowers it only while `amount < energy` and recharges 100/s, so the dip from a pulse (15 of 25,000) is gone in 0.15 s and nothing reads it; recorded in the row and FIDELITY.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: the hit target, today `HitRecord.target: EntityId` and `HitTarget.id`.
Decision: no-change for the target noun, add-alongside for the hit part. The `EntityId` namespace already names every new candidate: the statics have fixed ids 3 to 5 (`ids.ts`), pods and plates are store entities with dynamic ids, and the player is 100, so `HitRecord.target` stays an `EntityId` resolved through `STATIC_TARGETS` before the store. What a subentity hit adds is not a new target but which part of the target the beam ended on, so `firstShipHit` gains `part: HitPart` (`{ kind: "hull" }` or `{ kind: "subentity"; index }`) beside the id; the ledger receives only an amount already decided at queue time (plan 18-05's frangible bleed-through).
What would force a promote: a destructible subentity with its own tracked energy (a frangible buoy subentity that can die, Oolite's `subEntityDied`), which would need a `(target, part)` pair in `HitRecord` and per-subentity health in the snapshot (an owner-approved amendment of the frozen v4 field set).
Invariant test (Task 1): every `HitRecord.target` queued by `resolveShotHits` is `PLAYER_ID`, an id in `STATIC_TARGETS`, or a live store id at queue time.
</assumption_delta_decision>
