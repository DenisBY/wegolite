<objective>
Fix the two colour/lighting defects that make Lave read as a washed-out lime sphere: the authored sRGB palette is currently consumed as linear light and then encoded to sRGB again, and the terminator is a symmetric scalar smoothstep with a 0.1 ambient floor instead of Oolite's per-channel threshold.

Purpose: G-03-1 is the phase's remaining visual gap. Its root cause is a colour-space contract error plus a terminator that lifts the geometric day/night edge to 55% brightness. Both are measurable in 8-bit values, so they can be fixed and pinned without a screenshot.

Output: `src/render/color.ts` (one sRGB transfer), decoded palette uniforms and the per-channel terminator in `src/render/bodies/planet.ts` + `src/render/shaders/planet.glsl.ts`, and the tests that pin the authored albedo bytes.

**Scope boundary:** this plan changes colour and light only. The surface generator (coastline, sea depth, snowline, polar ice) is plan 03-11 and rewrites the same shader afterwards; keep the existing land/sea/cloud field untouched here so the two changes stay independently reviewable. Sun size, star density and the station are explicitly out of scope (the debug session eliminated them as causes).
</objective>
