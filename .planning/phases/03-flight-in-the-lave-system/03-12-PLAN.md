<objective>
Replace the station's ad-hoc lighting with one scene-wide exposure contract and light the Coriolis to Oolite's measured brightness, including its authored emissive.

Purpose: G-03-1's fourth root cause. The planet and the station are currently lit by two unrelated energy scales: the planet by hand-rolled shader maths, the station by `DirectionalLight 2.5 + AmbientLight 0.1` with `emissiveIntensity 0.8`, which puts lit Coriolis faces at 60-90/255 against Oolite's ~190/255. Fixing only the planet would leave a bright planet and a black station.

Chosen contract — calibrated LDR, not ACES. The alternatives were tested numerically: enabling `ACESFilmicToneMapping` and re-gaining the planet to keep its palette is possible but moves Lave's land and sea by several 8-bit levels and compresses the polar whites by ~20 levels, whereas the LDR path reproduces the authored palette *exactly* (03-10's pin) and still reaches Oolite's station brightness with a single light constant. Clipping is bounded: at the reference NdotL only ~1-4% of the brightest hull/faceplate texels saturate, and 0% at the sub-solar mean. The contract therefore is: linear light, no tone map, one sRGB encode, exposure 1 — with the station gain derived from Oolite's constants and the planet gain implicitly 1 (its albedo is already display-linear).

Output: `src/render/exposure.ts`, the `boot.ts` / `system-scene.ts` / `station.ts` wiring, and the numeric tests that replace "looks about right".

**Depends on 03-10** for `src/render/color.ts` (`srgbByteToLinear` / `linearToSrgb`) and for the planet-side pin this plan must not disturb. **Sequenced after 03-11** (wave 3) because both plans' full-suite verifies run `tests/render` and `tests/app` while each rewrites files the other owns (`03-11` rewrites `tests/render/planet-shader.test.ts`, this plan rewrites `tests/app/dispose.test.ts`); the dependency is declared rather than left implicit so the same-wave rule cannot be relied on by accident. Nothing in this plan's logic needs 03-11's surface code.
</objective>
