# Phase 3: Flight in the Lave System - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-03
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Deliver the first playable in-system flight loop: a new game starts docked at Lave's Coriolis, the player launches, and flies the Cobra Mk III through a faithfully scaled and stably rendered Lave system with pitch/roll flight, torus drive, and the v1 flight HUD.

In scope:

- Lave system scene: witchpoint, planet, sun, Coriolis station at faithful relative positions and scale.
- Stable rendering across the full ~1.4×10^6 m system extent, with a clear no-WebGL2 fallback.
- Launch flow with the +10 minute clock advance and launch roll.
- Aristotelian flight: pitch/roll only, ramped rates, 350 top speed, 160 u/s² thrust, no drift.
- Torus drive with mass-lock and pilot-input disengagement.
- Flight HUD: crosshair, scanner, compass, aegis, bars, clock, message line, condition light.
- Pause, rebinding, and locally persisted settings.
- Action/command input layer and a `ShipController` seam for later combat/NPC ships.
- Scene teardown with no GPU-resource leak.

Out of scope for this phase:

- Docking approach, dock capture and docking damage (Phase 4).
- Trading, outfitting, market screens, economy simulation (Phase 5).
- Hyperspace jumps and the galaxy chart (Phase 6).
- Combat, NPC traffic, missions, OXPs (not in v1).
- Planet landing, external/aft views, mouse flight, mid-flight saves (deferred beyond v1).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Planet and sky visuals

- **D-01:** Lave's planet surface is generated by a procedural GLSL material using the compiled planetinfo values already available in `src/content/generated/systems/g0.json` — `land_color`, `sea_color`, polar colours, `land_fraction`, `cloud_fraction`, `cloud_alpha`, `air_color`, `air_density`, `ambient_level` and a noise field seeded from `random_seed`. No baked planet texture and no new asset-pipeline step.
- **D-02:** A single planet material handles surface colour, clouds, a thin air-colour rim, and the day/night terminator. Do not add separate cloud or atmosphere shells in Phase 3; this keeps one draw call and avoids transparent-depth interactions with the station.
- **D-03:** The sun is an emissive sphere with an additive corona billboard driven by `corona_flare`, `corona_hues` and `corona_shimmer`. The starfield is deterministic, sized from `sky_n_stars`, seeded by `random_seed`, and tinted by `sun_color`. No baked skybox per system.
- **D-04:** The scene includes a witchpoint beacon at the arrival point, a nav buoy in front of the Coriolis dock, and camera-relative dust particles for speed cues. These are in addition to the planet, sun and Coriolis. Dust is the primary visual feedback that torus drive is engaged.

### HUD and scanner

- **D-05:** The flight HUD is drawn on a transparent Canvas2D overlay above the WebGL canvas. Use it for vector lines, the scanner ellipse and monospace text; no DOM/SVG HUD and no in-scene WebGL HUD geometry.
- **D-06:** The HUD follows Oolite's classic green-vector layout and information hierarchy: centre crosshair, elliptical scanner with vertical tails at the bottom, compass, aegis indicator, speed/roll/pitch and fuel bars, clock, condition light, and message line. This is the v1 subset from FLGT-05; do not add shields, energy, temperature, missiles, target reticle or MFDs.
- **D-07:** The scanner is a 3D elliptical display with vertical tails and a 25.6 km range. In the traffic-free v1 it plots only the Coriolis station, nav buoy and witchpoint beacon when they are in range. Planets and suns are deliberately excluded, matching Oolite's scanner behaviour and avoiding a dominant planet blob.
- **D-08:** Lay the HUD out once in a fixed 1920×1080 design space, then uniformly scale to fit the window with letterboxed margins. Do not reflow or edge-anchor for ultrawide; preserve classic proportions. Render at device pixel ratio, capped as the existing renderer does.

### Launch experience

- **D-09:** A new game boots through a minimal title/start screen, then starts docked at Lave's Coriolis. The docked view is a single minimal status panel showing system name, credits (100.0 Cr), ship (Cobra Mk III), fuel (7.0 LY), and a Launch action. No trade, outfitting, market or mission screens in this phase.
- **D-10:** Launch plays a short scripted exit of about 2 seconds: break-pattern/tunnel effect, the Cobra sliding out of the slot with the station's roll (`launchRoll = -station roll`), then control handover. The sequence is skippable on any key after the first viewing. Advance the clock by +10 minutes exactly once at launch.
- **D-11:** Teach first use through the HUD message line only: a launch hint while docked, then torus and speed hints once in flight. Each hint is dismissed after its first successful use. No modal tutorial and no permanent controls overlay.

### Controls and settings

- **D-12:** Every action implemented in Phase 3 is rebindable through a semantic action map: pitch forward/back, roll left/right, speed increase/decrease, torus, pause, launch, target station, compass cycle, and any other action added by the phase. Include conflict detection and reset-to-defaults.
- **D-13:** Control bindings and preferences persist as one versioned JSON blob in `localStorage`. Do not put settings in IndexedDB; IndexedDB is reserved for game saves in later phases.
- **D-14:** The pause overlay is the single home for Resume, Controls (rebinding) and Settings. It freezes the simulation, is reachable both docked and in flight, and is opened with `p` or `Esc`.
- **D-15:** On window blur and on `visibilitychange` to hidden, auto-pause the simulation and clear all held input. This prevents stuck keys and clock drift, matching Oolite's focus-loss behaviour.

### the agent's Discretion

- Exact noise function, octave count and mesh tessellation for the procedural planet, provided Lave's colours, land fraction, cloud coverage and seeded variation are recognisable.
- Exact green palette, font stack, line weights and scanner projection maths, provided the classic layout and 1920×1080 design space are respected.
- Dust particle count, size, lifetime and reseeding strategy; the only requirement is that it reads as motion, especially under torus.
- Corona billboard shape, falloff and additive blending details.
- Launch animation easing and exact 2-second timing within the "short and skippable" decision.
- Default key assignments, conflict-resolution UX, and the shape of the rebinding capture flow.
- Torus ramp and mass-lock *feedback* details (message-line text, dust intensity), using the researched formula; the requirement itself fixes 32×–1024×, station/planet mass-lock, and pilot-input disengagement.
- Module layout for `sim/flight`, `render/bodies`, `render/hud`, `input` and `ui`, provided the existing layering and sim-purity rules hold.
- Test mechanics for scene disposal and WebGL2 fallback, provided WORLD-04 and WORLD-05 are demonstrably covered.

</decisions>

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets

- `cameraRelative()` and the `place()` convention in `src/main.ts`: every render object is positioned by float64 subtraction from the camera, with the camera fixed at the origin. Reuse this for all new bodies, dust and HUD-projected markers.
- `src/render/precision-scene.ts`: deterministic golden-angle star placement, camera-locked shell, and the near/far marker patterns can seed the starfield and distant-body rendering.
- `src/sim/loop.ts` + `src/sim/step.ts`: fixed-step harness, backlog clamping and canonical state hashing are ready to extend rather than replace.
- `src/sim/state.ts` + `src/sim/hash.ts`: labelled canonical serialization and FNV-1a hashing support numeric golden tests for flight curves and cadence equality.
- `src/content/systems.ts` and `galaxy0()`: Lave is already eager and typed; no new content loading path is needed for this phase.
- Phase 2 GLBs and textures are already in `public/`; the Coriolis hull, faceplate, tris and dock are separate nodes, so station assembly is a scene-graph task, not a new asset task.

### Established Patterns

- `src/sim/` is pure TypeScript and must not import Three.js or touch the DOM; the guard test enforces the layer boundary.
- The camera sits at the origin and only rotates; world positions stay float64 and are converted immediately before upload. Near plane 0.5, far plane 2,500,000, logarithmic depth buffer on.
- Fixed timestep is 1/120 s with at most 5 steps per frame and a 0.25 s frame-delta clamp. Flight state changes only inside the fixed step.
- Content is compiled at build time into JSON modules; the sim imports only types. Runtime validates the manifest, not every content record.
- Existing probe global `window.__wegolite` exposes renderer/precision facts for Playwright; extend rather than replace it for flight/HUD/disposal verification.
- Generated content stores raw Oolite units. Convert at the presentation boundary: `radiusKm * 10` for game units and displayed tech level `techLevelRaw + 1`.

### Integration Points

- `src/main.ts` is currently a single boot/render file. Phase 3 should introduce an app shell with `MenuScene`/`SystemScene`, `input/`, `ui/` and `render/hud/`, while keeping the existing renderer and camera-relative contract.
- `src/sim/step.ts` probe constants (`PROBE_PITCH_RATE`, `PROBE_ROLL_RATE`, `PROBE_MAX_SPEED`, `PROBE_THRUST_ACCEL`) are explicitly placeholders and must be replaced by data-derived flight constants.
- `src/sim/state.ts` currently has only `tick`, `position`, `velocity`, `orientation`; add flight speed/target, clock, mode, station pose and controller-facing state without importing content values.
- The full ship catalogue lives in generated JSON; the Cobra player template is the source of flight stats, but the catalogue variant entry (`cobra3-alternate`) differs, so verify `oolite_template_cobra3-player` before deriving constants.
- The existing Playwright probe and Docker `verify` chain are the places to attach flight, HUD, fallback and dispose assertions; no screenshot/pixel-diff evidence.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Lave geometry: `radiusKm = 4116` → 41,160 game units; `planetDistance = 452,760`; `sunDistance = 905,520`; `sunRadius = 84,449.095459`; station position = planet centre − `stationVector × 2 × planetRadius`; worst-case view extent ≈ 1.4×10^6 m.
- Flight constants: max flight speed 350 u/s, thrust 32 × 5 = 160 u/s², max pitch 1 rad/s, max roll 2 rad/s. Pitch/roll ramp at 2 × max rate and damp at 1.0 rad/s²; reversal is faster. No yaw and no Newtonian drift.
- Torus: `j` toggles; multiplier starts at 32× and rises as `32 × sqrt(distance / (4 × 25,600))`, capped at 1024×; mass-lock near station/planet; any pilot input disengages.
- Starting state: Lave (`g0-s7`), Cobra Mk III, 100.0 Cr (1000 decicredits), 7.0 LY fuel, 20 t cargo hold, forward pulse laser, three cosmetic missiles. Lave is Rich Agricultural, Dictatorship, raw TL 4 (displayed 5).
- Coriolis station roll is 0.08 rad/s and spins about its local Z axis, not Y. `launchRoll = -station roll`.
- Scanner range is 25,600 units. HUD design space is 1920×1080. Clock advances +10 minutes at launch.
- The planet must use Oolite's planetinfo palette and noise seed, not a generic Earth-like blue marble. Planet scale is intentionally 1:100; do not rescale to real kilometres.
- Give the coplanar Coriolis layers (hull/faceplate/tris/dock) explicit `polygonOffset` or render order; the dock sits 0.2561 units outside the hull and is the main z-fighting risk.
- Clear all held input on blur, `visibilitychange`, pointer-lock change and fullscreen change, even with auto-pause.

</specifics>
