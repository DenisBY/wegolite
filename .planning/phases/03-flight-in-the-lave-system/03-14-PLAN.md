<objective>
Cut the planet's per-pixel fragment cost from 16 to 10 worst-case value-noise evaluations (9 if the pre-agreed slow-run step runs), about 9.3 average (8.3 on the 9-evaluation path), while keeping the approved Lave surface, palette and relief shape, so the full 29-test browser suite goes green in its 2-worker configuration with at least 1.3x headroom, and add a deterministic guard that fails if the budget ever grows again.

Purpose: G-03-3. The phase-03 gap closure raised the shader from 7 to 16 noise evaluations per pixel. Under headless SwiftShader (4 CPUs, 2 Playwright workers) that is 4.3 fps solo and 1.91-1.96 fps per page under load. The game loop deliberately caps catch-up at 5 x 1/120 s, so simulated time advances at exactly `fps x 5/120` (0.18x wall solo, 0.080-0.082x loaded). The launch/hint queue needs 12.92 s of simulated time, which is 161.5 s of wall time under load — past the test's fixed 150 s wait. Raising the wait or serialising workers would hide a 2.3x throughput regression; restoring the shader budget fixes it.

Purpose (how the budget is chosen): the two measurements also fix the cost model. The pre-gap 7-evaluation shader ran at about 8 fps solo (125 ms per frame); the current shader measured 4.29 fps solo (233 ms per frame) at **16** evaluations per pixel (surface 6 + two 3-octave relief taps + cloud 4) — the measured build's relief taps are not sea-gated, so the gate this plan introduces must not be used to discount that point. A linear fit through (7, 125 ms) and (16, 233 ms) is about 41 ms fixed per frame plus 12.0 ms per average evaluation. With the measured loaded/solo ratio 0.445-0.457, the 10-evaluation build (average 9.3) models 6.6 fps solo, 2.9-3.0 fps loaded, sim rate 0.121-0.125, drain 104-106 s and duration 128-130 s — 1.41-1.45x headroom on the 150 s wait. At 11 evaluations the same fit gives about 2.7 fps loaded, a 112-115 s drain and 1.30-1.34x — at the gate, with no margin against the fit's own two-point error. That is why the plan starts at 10 and keeps 9 as the pre-agreed step. The fit is indicative; the measured full-suite drain, not the model, is the decisive number.

Purpose (why this arithmetic and not "24 fps"): the test does not need real-time play, it needs the drain to fit. `12.92 / 150 = 0.086` sim rate, i.e. 2.07 fps under load, is the absolute floor; the gate is 1.3x above that floor, a derived drain of at most 115 s. G-03-3's `missing[0]` ("rendered cadence >= 24 fps") is restated as this drain arithmetic in the SUMMARY, because the loop obeys `sim rate = fps x 5/120` and 24 fps was never the requirement.

Output: the reduced-budget shader, the mirror constants that keep the calibration honest, a re-measured `RELIEF_SCALE`, and `tests/render/planet-budget.test.ts`.
</objective>
