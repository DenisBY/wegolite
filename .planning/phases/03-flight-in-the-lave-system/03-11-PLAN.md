<objective>
Replace the coarse 4-octave value-noise blob with a calibrated PlanetMix surface: coastlines whose land share matches the authored `land_fraction`, deep sea darkening, a coastal shelf, a snowline, Lambert terrain relief, and polar ice driven by the authored ice fraction instead of a latitude-only wash.

Purpose: G-03-1's second half. The diagnosis measured the current field at 87.4% land against an intended 65%, with no sea depth, no relief and a latitude-only ice wash — which is why Lave reads as soft lime blobs rather than an Oolite planet.

Purpose (why calibration, not a constant): the normalised octave sum is not uniformly distributed, so `1 - land_fraction` is the wrong threshold by construction. The fix is to measure the field the shader actually evaluates and cut it at the matching quantile, deterministically, from the compiled seed.

Output: `src/render/planet-noise.ts` (a testable CPU mirror plus the calibration), the PlanetMix shader, the wiring in `planet.ts`, the optional `percent_ice` schema key, and `tests/render/planet-surface.test.ts`.
