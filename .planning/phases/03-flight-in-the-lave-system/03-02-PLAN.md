**D-10 sign reconciliation (recorded here so no reviewer reads an inversion into the plans).** D-10 writes `launchRoll = -station roll`. That is Oolite's *authored* basis: Oolite's `launchRoll` is the ship-relative roll the scripted launch applies in the entity frame, whose sign is negative relative to the station's world roll once the entity sits in the slot. Wegolite does not carry a separate relative roll field — it gives the ship the *world* roll rate `stationRollRadPerSec` (`0.12566370614359174` rad/s, positive about its local `+Z`, the same axis and sign as `stationAngle`), which is the physical fact Oolite's authored sign encodes: the ship rolls *with* the slot, so the station-relative angle stays ~0. Both descriptions are therefore the same motion in different bases, and the implemented sign is `+stationRollRadPerSec` for a steady slot (research A2 / Open Question 1). Task 1 step 4 is the executable statement of that reconciliation: it passes only for the world-sense sign and fails both the no-roll and the doubled counter-rotation cases. Do not "fix" the sign toward the literal `-` on the strength of D-10's shorthand.
