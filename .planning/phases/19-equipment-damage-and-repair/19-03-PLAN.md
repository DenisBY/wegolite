## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-11:** the damaged item lives only in `damagedEquipment`, never as an `_DAMAGED` key; the two lists together stay within the 64-key bound. Durability comes from the existing autosave triggers; nothing here writes a save.
- **D-03:** the one damageable set, `DAMAGEABLE_EQUIPMENT_KEYS`: Oolite's rule over what Wegolite can install, so every key the roll can damage and the commit can record is one the repair door (19-05) repairs and the save (19-08) accepts; no laser record is in it (checker iteration 3).
- **D-04:** the cargo removal operation the app commits for a destroyed pod.
- **D-06:** Oolite's in-use behaviour: the compass fallback and the dock computer refusal hold through the installed set (this plan); the autopilot disengage, the galactic-jump abort and the ANA clause have no in-use state in Wegolite (instant Shift+C dock, no galactic jump, no ANA consumer; research audit) and are recorded as not applicable today, with a tripwire, in 19-15.
- **Reversibility:** reversible: no persisted format changes in this plan (`profileFromSave` reads a v4 save, whose format cannot carry damage, so its restored profile has an empty damaged list until 19-08 replaces the reader).

Accepted debt: the two lists must stay disjoint and within one combined bound; the companion invariant test (`tests/sim/player/damage.property.test.ts`, extended with the repair operation in 19-05) goes red if a later operation breaks that.
