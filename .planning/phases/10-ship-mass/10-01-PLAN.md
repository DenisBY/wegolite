<objective>
Port the construction and volume parts of Oolite's hull octree (`OOMeshToOctreeConverter.m`, `Octree.m`, the depth rule of `OOMesh.m`) into the content tooling, and compose Oolite's ship mass from it, so that every ship the inert world uses gets the mass Oolite computes (DATA-11, D-01, D-02).

Purpose: Phase 12 needs per-ship masses for mass lock and jump blockage, and D-04 needs the computed Coriolis mass to compare with 455,223,200. This plan builds and proves the computation; plan 10-02 writes the numbers into the compiled records. The octree exists only at build time (D-05, roadmap criterion 4): nothing here is imported by `src/`, and runtime ray and hull queries (DATA-16) are not built.

Claude's discretion from CONTEXT, resolved by the 10-RESEARCH.md spike: float32 at every operation (bit-identical to a C float32 build on all 16 meshes; float64 changes nothing structural and moves the volume by at most 2.03e-7 relative); Oolite's own adaptive depth rule and volume limit of 5; density from `shipdata.plist` with Oolite's default 1.0; module layout `octree.ts` (geometry, no catalogue) plus `ship-mass.ts` (composition from the catalogue). Stated tolerances: golden values bit-exact float32; worked examples against an independent float64 derivation within relative 1e-6; the community table to the whole tonne.

The roadmap formula is incomplete: Oolite also adds the mass of each solid (non-flasher) subentity (`ShipEntity.m:2034-2041`, `mass += [subent mass]`). That changes the Coriolis from 228,650,880 (hull) to 515,483,904 and the buoy from 691,044.375 to 698,691.1875; the role ships and the Cobra have no mesh subentity. Criterion 2's "reproduce Oolite's mass" is defined as stated in the fourth must-have truth, because there is no Oolite binary to run here.

Output: two tool modules and their two test files. No generated file changes in this plan.
</objective>
