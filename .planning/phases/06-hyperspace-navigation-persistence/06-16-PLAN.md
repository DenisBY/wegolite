<objective>
**As a** player, **I want to** be refused a witchspace jump that would complete while my ship is still inside the station's mass-blockage radius, and to be told why, **so that** I must fly clear of the station before a jump can leave its vicinity, as in Oolite.

Purpose: NAV-04 gains Oolite's blockage refusal at countdown completion. Oolite runs the blockage clause only while the status is the countdown, and it checks at the countdown's end (PlayerEntity.m lines 6547 and 6772-6775). The H press also calls the checklist, but the clause is inert then (PlayerEntityControls.m lines 1531-1532). This plan follows Oolite: the blockage is evaluated once, at completion. It is not evaluated at the H press, because a refusal there would stop a pilot from starting a countdown and flying out during the 15 seconds, which Oolite allows.

Output: the blocked refusal reason, the recorded Coriolis mass, the blockage predicate and the completion branch (countdown.ts and step.ts); the per-step mass fact and the Witchspace jump aborted copy (game-session.ts and probe.ts); a unit file for the boundaries and the completion path; browser fly-away steps that use real throttle input in the two specs that commit a jump, and a blocked browser case; the PROJECT.md row for the Coriolis blockage, beneath the superseded row 136; and the D-07 amendment in CONTEXT.md.

Requirement mapping: NAV-04 (a 15 second abortable countdown, and a completed jump advances the clock). A blocked completion is not a completed jump.

Gap identifiers: gap_ids is empty by design. This plan closes no declared gap in 06-GAP-CLOSURE.md or 06-VERIFICATION.md, and it adds no ledger row. Its source is the owner-directed blockage decision, recorded as a new PROJECT.md row beneath row 136. Row 136's Outcome cell is marked superseded, and its Decision and Rationale cells stay as history.

Station mass, corrected premise: the owner's note says the Coriolis mass comes from the Oolite config. That does not hold. shipdata.plist has no mass key (its one occurrence of the word is a comment about pack holds), the vendored whitelist names mass only as an allowed key, no vendored file or ingest step carries a mass value, and Oolite computes a ship's mass at runtime from its density and model octree volume (ShipEntity.m lines 419 and 1077). The figure 455,223,200 appears only in a comment in checkShipsInVicinityForWitchJumpExit (ShipEntity.m line 12504). The vendor script that would add data downloads from the network, which AGENTS.md forbids. So this plan records the figure as a constant with its citation, the same way arrival.ts records MIN_DISTANCE_TO_BUOY, and supplies it per step as a read-only fact for Coriolis systems only. The predicate has no docked parameter; see the docked point below.

Owner decision 2026-11-07, option (a): the 224 dodecahedron (167 systems) and icosahedron (57 systems) stations have no recorded mass. They stay non-blocking. This plan invents no mass for them. The data gap is the reason: the owner decided that these kinds do not block until their data exists. That is recorded in PROJECT.md as the owner's decision, not as a design choice.

Navigation buoy, a data gap and not a design choice: Oolite's blockage loop considers every ship except the jumping ship itself (ShipEntity.m line 12520). The navigation buoy is a ship. Its definition is in the vendored config: shipdata.plist line 1011 defines oolite_template_buoy, with model oolite_buoy.dat at line 1026 and roles buoy at line 1028, and shiplibrary.plist line 53 lists ship buoy. Its template block has no density key (shipdata.plist lines 1011-1096), so Oolite's default density of 1.0 applies (ShipEntity.m lines 417 and 1075). Oolite computes mass as density times 20 times the model's octree volume (ShipEntity.m lines 419 and 1077). The input that is missing is therefore the octree volume. It needs the buoy model oolite_buoy.dat, which is not in third_party/oolite/Models, a directory that holds only the cobra3 and coriolis models. The buoy is non-blocking for lack of recorded data, not by design. It stays non-blocking until its model is vendored and its octree volume computed. That is the same class of data gap as the 224 non-Coriolis stations.

Docked jumper: a docked ship never reaches completion. A dock cancels the countdown (plan 06-08, and the same-frame case in plan 06-13), and a docked begin is inert. Oolite's clause at ShipEntity.m line 12527 excludes a docked player only when the player is the candidate blocker. It is not an exemption for a docked jumper, and a Wegolite jumper is never a candidate. For that reason the predicate takes no docked parameter, and the unit file pins the docked begin instead.

Ordering: this plan is wave 4 and depends on 06-13, 06-14 and 06-15. Plan 06-15 depends on 06-17 (the chart orientation plan), so the three plans that share game-session.ts, tests/e2e/hyperspace.spec.ts and PROJECT.md run in the order 06-17, 06-15, 06-16. This plan does not change 06-17's content.

Framing source: the story above comes from the owner's direction and the v1 omission row in PROJECT.md. The ROADMAP phase Goal line is not written as a user story, so this plan does not derive its framing from it.

Tracer note: the leading tracer is the sim path from the H press through the 1,800-step countdown to the blocked refusal event, verified by a runnable end-to-end unit run, with the app copy and probe typing in the same task. The browser proofs are the next two tasks, because the five-file limit keeps the sim and app layers in separate tasks.

Tests and builds run in Docker only (AGENTS.md). Host Node, npm and Playwright are not evidence. The J-drive mass lock (speed.ts and torus.ts) is a different mechanism and must not change.
</objective>
