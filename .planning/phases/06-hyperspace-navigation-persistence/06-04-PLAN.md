| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-11 | Data loss | arrival/launch autosave over a future or unreadable blob | high | mitigate | Every write goes through `canOverwrite(saveStatus)` and `queueAutosave`'s `isEncodedSaveValid`; future/unreadable blobs stay byte-identical and the skip reason is published. |
