# Asset provenance

No asset may be committed to this repository without a row in the table below.
That includes every Oolite-derived 3D model, texture, image, sound and
configuration file, and it is the record `AGENTS.md` and `NOTICE` refer to.

Columns: the repository-relative path, what kind of asset it is, where it came
from, the upstream URL, the author or copyright holder, the licence it is taken
under, the modifications Wegolite made to it, and the SHA-256 of the committed
file. Oolite-derived assets are recorded as CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (attribution
required, non-commercial only, share-alike).
